Direct messages are private conversations between your team and one client in your app. They're free, they support attachments, and clients reply right in the app.

## Who can get a direct message

Anyone in your club who has signed in to your app, on the web or the mobile app, can get a direct message. In the recipient list, someone who hasn't signed in yet shows **Not on the app yet**, with **Text instead** or **Email instead** so you can still reach them. See [Clients who aren't on the app yet](/inbox/new-message#clients-who-arent-on-the-app-yet).

## Send a direct message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a new direct message">
    In **Inbox**, click **New message**, then **Direct message** under **In the app**. To reply to someone you've already talked to, just open their conversation and use the reply box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who gets it">
    In **Add clients or tags**, pick people by name, or pick a tag, team or role to message a group. Each person gets their own private conversation. See [Send a new message](/inbox/new-message).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your message">
    Write in **Message** (up to 15,000 characters). A web address you paste becomes a link the client can tap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach something (optional)">
    Click **Attach** to add a form, media, a product or a scheduler. See [Message attachments](/inbox/attaching-forms-schedulers-and-products).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send message**. For a group, check the review step first, then click **Send to** and the number of clients.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How clients get it

- **Mobile app:** a push notification, and the message in their **Direct Message** inbox.
- **Web app:** the message in their Direct Message inbox.
- **Email:** an email notification, so they don't miss it if they haven't opened the app.

In the client's Direct Message inbox, each run of messages from your team shows the staff member's name and initials, so families know who they're talking to. Day headings (Today, Yesterday) and times match what you see in your Inbox. Files you attach show as tiles; a document such as a .csv or Word file shows its name and type with a **Download file** button.

## Replies

Replies land in your Inbox under **Open** (and **In-app**). Every staff member with Inbox access can see and answer them. To keep track of who's handling what, see [Work through conversations as a team](/inbox/work-as-a-team).

### Messages CoachIQ sends for a client

One kind of direct message is written by CoachIQ, not the client. When an athlete tries to book with credits and is short, CoachIQ sends you a message from their account asking for credits, along the lines of: "Hi! I'd like to book [scheduler] on [date · time], but I'm 1 credit short. Could you add credits to my account? Thanks!"

Treat it as a booking request. Add the credits (use **Add credits** in the [Profile panel](/inbox/client-profile-and-activity#profile), or see [Managing athlete credits](/payments/credits/managing-athlete-credits)), then reply so they know to book.

## Unsend a direct message

1. Open the conversation and click the message's actions menu, beside its time.
2. Choose **Unsend** and confirm.

The message disappears for everyone, on the web and in the mobile app, and stays gone after a reload. Admins can unsend any direct message in their group. Coaches, parents and athletes can unsend only their own. Unsend doesn't recall a text or email.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I see if a client read my message?">
    No. Direct messages don't show read receipts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do group direct messages show everyone's replies?">
    No. When you send to several people, each person has a private conversation with your team. Each one also shows under that person in your Inbox.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can parents get direct messages?">
    Yes, once the parent has signed in to your app. Pick the parent by name, or pick a team to include parents automatically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can clients send files back?">
    Clients can attach photos and videos to their messages.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
