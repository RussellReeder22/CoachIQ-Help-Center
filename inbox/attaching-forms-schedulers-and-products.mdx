Attachments turn a message into an action: a waiver to sign, a video to watch, a membership to buy or a session to book.

## What you can attach

| Attachment | What the client gets |
| --- | --- |
| **Forms** | A form from your Forms list, such as a waiver or registration, to fill in |
| **Media** | A video, image, audio file or document from your [Media Hub](/content/media-overview), or a file you upload |
| **Product** | A product they can buy, with its price |
| **Scheduler** | A scheduler they can book from, with its open times |

Attachments work in direct messages, announcements and texts. Emails have their own **Image** and **PDF** buttons; see [Email](/inbox/email#add-images).

## Add an attachment

1. In a new message, click **Attach** under the message. In a conversation, click **+** in the reply box (it also holds **Emoji**).
2. Choose **Forms**, **Media**, **Product** or **Scheduler**.
3. Pick one or more items. For **Media**, choose files already in your Media Hub, or upload a file from your device; an uploaded file is also saved to your Media Hub.
4. Your attachments show under the message. Send as usual.

You can mix types in one message. Keep it to three or four attachments and say in the message what each one is for.

## How clients see attachments

### In the app

Attachments show as cards under the message:

- **Forms** show the form's name. Long names wrap onto two lines.
- **Sessions** show the name, price and length, for example "60 min · \$50", or "Free".
- **Products** show the name and price.
- **Media** shows pictures and videos as previews, audio with play controls, and documents (PDF, Word, Excel, .csv, .zip) as a file tile with the name and type. Opening a document offers **Download file**.

Several attachments wrap onto new lines on a phone, so every one can be tapped.

### In texts

Attachments go out as links. A text to one person puts each item's name before its link ("Waiver Form: [link]"). Group texts list the links without names. Links count towards the text's length.

### In announcement emails

Each product shows as a card with its name, price (for example "\$300.00 / month"), a short description and a **Purchase** button, so families can tell similar memberships apart. Forms, sessions and media show their names with **Open form**, **Book** or **View**. Archived or deleted products are left out. Announcements posted by an automation get the same cards.

## Things to know

- **Forms:** responses appear under the form's **Responses**. A form can be set up so clients don't need to sign in to fill it in.
- **Schedulers:** the link always shows current open times. Check the scheduler has times open before you send it.
- **Products:** the card opens the product so clients can buy it.
- **Editing an announcement** keeps its attachments unless you remove them.

### When a shared file is deleted

A sent attachment depends on the original. If you delete a Media Hub file, it's removed from every message it was sent in, including archived conversations. Media Hub warns you about affected messages before you confirm. Clients then see **Attachment no longer available**. If you still have the file, upload it again and send it as a new attachment; that doesn't repair the old message. The same goes for a deleted form, product or scheduler.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can clients attach files in their replies?">
    Clients can attach photos and videos to their direct messages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I see who opened an attachment?">
    Not link by link. Form submissions show under the form's responses, purchases in Transactions, and bookings on your calendar.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I send a PDF?">
    Yes. Attach it from **Media** in a direct message or announcement, or use **PDF** in an email to send it as an email attachment.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
