Messaging credits pay for outbound SMS and coach-written emails in CoachIQ. Both use your organization's shared credit balance, including the email copies of announcements.

<Info>
  **Which credits?** These are your business's **messaging credits**, also called SMS credits. They are completely separate from the **session credits** athletes buy and redeem for bookings. See [Credit system overview](/payments/credits/credit-system-overview) for those.
</Info>

## What a credit is

A credit is a prepaid unit of usage. At the standard rate, **$1 buys 100 credits**, and a short plain-text message costs **about 1 credit**.

## What uses credits

Credits are spent on **outbound** texts: messages you send from the Inbox, mass SMS to a tag, automation SMS actions, and Zapier SMS actions. Incoming replies do not use credits.

Coach-written emails use the same balance at **0.1 credit per email address**: Inbox emails, announcement email copies, and automation **Send an Email** actions, including CC and BCC. Automatic notifications such as booking confirmations and receipts are free. See [Email](/inbox/email#what-email-costs).

### SMS costs

SMS costs are per **segment**, and one text can be several segments:

| Message | Segments | Approximate cost |
|---|---|---|
| Plain text, up to about 160 characters | 1 | 1 credit |
| Plain text, 161 to about 300 characters | 2 | 2 credits |
| Any message containing an emoji or special character | 1 per ~70 characters | 2 to 3 credits for a typical text |
| A mass text to 200 athletes | 1 per recipient | 200 credits or more |

<Tip>
  Keep texts short and skip emojis. An emoji switches the whole message to a different encoding that cuts each segment to about 70 characters, so one friendly 🎉 can double or triple the cost of a broadcast.
</Tip>

### Email costs

At the default rate, **10 emails use 1 credit**. A send to 37 email addresses uses **3.7 credits**; fractions carry forward, so each email is not rounded up to a whole credit. CC and BCC addresses count too.

On the **Credits** page, **Used this month** includes an **Email** line. The activity log shows each send and its credit use. This helps explain a balance change even when you have not sent any texts.

## How you get credits

- **Monthly allotment** — your plan may include a monthly grant of credits. It is added to whatever balance you have left; it does not reset it.
- **Top-ups** — buy more at any time, either a preset package (some include bonus credits) or a custom dollar amount. Payment goes through Stripe and the credits are applied automatically.
- **Auto-refill** — turn it on and your balance tops itself up when it runs low, to help keep enough credits available for messaging. See below.

<Note>
  Your balance, top-ups, auto-refill, and the credit activity log all live on the **Credits** page. Open it from **Settings → Communication → Credits**. It is the same page whether you get there from Settings or from the SMS setup flow in the Inbox, and it appears for every organization that pays for texts with credits.
</Note>

## Auto-refill

Auto-refill is the set-and-forget option. Once a card is on file, switch it on from the **Credits** page and CoachIQ buys more credits automatically whenever your balance drops low, charging the card on file and logging the purchase in the activity log like any other top-up.

- **Needs a card on file.** The control is unavailable until a payment method is saved, so add one first if you do not see it.
- **Turn it off any time** from the same page. Your remaining balance stays.
- **Check the activity log** if you are unsure whether a refill ran; each automatic purchase appears there with the credits added.

<Tip>
  Turn on auto-refill before a season of automated reminders or a big registration push. Running out mid-broadcast means part of the group gets the text and part does not.
</Tip>

## Texts aren't sending?

Two independent things must be true for a business text to deliver: your number must be **registered** with the carriers, and you must have **credits**. They fail differently.

1. **Check 10DLC registration first.** US carriers do not deliver business SMS until your brand and campaign are approved. This is the most common reason texts do not arrive, and it has nothing to do with your balance. See [Setting up SMS messaging](/inbox/sms-messages#setting-up-sms-messaging).
2. **Then check credits.** If your balance has run out, top up. Having credits does not make an unregistered number deliver, and being registered does not send texts you cannot pay for.

## Emails or announcement copies did not arrive?

1. Check the balance in **Settings → Communication → Credits**. If a send is blocked with **Not enough credits to send this email**, add credits before trying again. An automation email blocked for this reason sends nothing and records the error in its execution log.
2. For an Inbox email, open **Channels → Email** and review the recipient addresses and delivery status. See [Find emails you've sent](/inbox/email#find-emails-youve-sent).
3. For an announcement, check the email delivery separately from the in-app post. If insufficient credits block its email copies, the announcement can still appear in the app. See [Announcement costs](/inbox/announcements#what-announcements-cost).

SMS carrier registration applies to texts. Email delivery has its own checks; a credit balance alone does not guarantee that a message reaches someone's inbox.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do incoming texts use credits?">
    No. Only messages you send are metered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a message to a tag cost one credit?">
    No. A broadcast is one segment per recipient. 150 athletes means at least 150 credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are SMS credits the same as athlete session credits?">
    No. Session credits are what athletes redeem for bookings and are managed on each athlete's profile. Messaging credits pay for outbound texts and coach-written emails. Different balances, different pages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see the auto-refill option?">
    It needs a card on file. Add a payment method on the **Credits** page first, then the auto-refill control becomes available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I see how many credits a campaign used?">
    Open the **Credits** page. The activity log lists each send with the credits deducted and the balance after.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
