These guides are for the coach who runs a business out of a training facility, or is on the way to one. They cover the decisions that happen away from the court: what to charge, how families find you, how to pick and lease a space, how to protect yourself, and how to bring on your first trainers. Each one draws on facility owners in the CoachIQ community and shows where the product does the work for you.

Read them in the order your business grows. The first two apply from day one, even if you're still renting gym time by the hour.
