**Where to find this:** website builder → **Add Section** → **Default Templates** → **Content + Media**
</Info>

A Content + Media section puts a photo or video next to a heading, a description and up to two buttons. Use it as the hero at the top of your home page, or further down to point families at a program, a camp or a free first session.

  ![A Content + Media section with a heading, description, button and video](/images/content-media-example.avif)
</Frame>

## Add the section

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Go to **Website & App** → **Website**, click **Edit website**, and pick the page from the page menu at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Content + Media">
    Click **Add Section**, open **Default Templates**, click **Content + Media**, then **Add to page**. The section lands at the bottom of the page; drag it where you want it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Click the section in the left panel. Replace the media, write the heading and description, and set the button's label and link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**, then check the live page on a computer and a phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Media** | The photo or video. Use **Replace Media**, **Upload New Media** or **Select from Media Hub**. For a video, add a **Thumbnail** image to show before it plays. Turn the switch off for a text-only section. |
| **Show Media Settings** | Opens the options below. |
| **Media Alt Text** | A short description of the image for screen readers and Google. |
| **Media Size** | How the media fits its frame: **Contain**, **Cover** or **Auto**. **Contain** shows the whole image; **Cover** fills the frame and crops the edges. |
| **Media Background** | The color behind the media, which shows when the image doesn't fill the frame. |
| **Flip Content & Media** | Swaps the sides, so the media sits on the left and the text on the right. |
| **Split Vertically** | Stacks the text above the media instead of side by side, centered on the page. |
| **Subheading** | A short line above the heading, often in capitals. |
| **Heading** | The section's title. Up to 500 characters. |
| **Description** | A line or paragraph under the heading. Up to 1,500 characters. |
| **Buttons** | The first button's label. Click the gear to choose where it goes. |
| **Second Button** | Turns on a second button next to the first, with its own label and link. |

For **Background**, **Vertical Padding** and width, turn on **Change Section Styles**. See [Backgrounds & Styling](/website/sections/backgrounds-styling). For button links, see [Buttons & CTAs](/website/sections/buttons-ctas).

## Tips

- Make the first button the one thing you most want a new family to do, such as booking a free evaluation, and send it straight there with the gear: **Schedule**, **Form** or **Product** under **CoachIQ Asset**. Use the second button for something lighter, such as **Member portal (login)** for families who already train with you.
- Video works well at the top of the home page. Add a **Thumbnail** so the section doesn't show a blank frame while the video loads.
