## Book without leaving your website

Sections linked to a CoachIQ scheduler can open its booking experience on your site. The scheduler controls available dates, session lengths, capacity, location, and payment requirements. Edit those settings in [Schedule → Schedulers](/scheduling/creating-your-first-scheduler); changing website copy alone does not change the booking rules.

After publishing, open the booking flow from the live section and check that it uses the intended scheduler.

### What visitors see when they book

- **Time zone:** booking calendars show your schedule's time zone, for example "Times shown in PDT", and repeat it next to the list of times. It's always your schedule's zone, not the visitor's. A week where the clocks change reads PDT/PST.
- **Guest booking:** when a session allows guests (see [Guest checkout](/payments/guest-checkout)), **Reserve My Spot** offers **Continue as guest** alongside sign in. Guests enter a first and last name and don't need a password. If the session needs an account, visitors are asked to sign in or create one. A visitor who creates an account partway through goes straight to payment for the time they picked, with their details filled in.
- **Credits:** a credit booking stops at a confirmation window that shows the athlete's available credits and what the booking uses before anything is spent. If they don't have enough, the window says how many more they need and offers **Add more credits** when the session has a credit pack link set.
- **Access-code sessions:** the calendar shows the same open days as your app. If no days are open, for example because the athlete reached a booking limit, the calendar says why.
- **Archived sessions:** if a section still points at a session that was archived or deleted, the calendar says "This session is not available." Pick an active session for the section and publish.
- **Book Now** and **Learn More** buttons that continue to your app open it in a new tab and show a small new-tab icon. Members already signed in on your website arrive signed in.

The multi-step booking form template uses wording that fits both free and paid sessions ("Book a Session", "Upcoming Session"), and the session card shows the real price. Wording you saved yourself is kept.

### **Introduction**

This feature is ideal for hosting events, running camps, and addressing various scheduling needs on your webpage. This guide will walk you through the process of adding and customizing a scheduling block, helping you make the most of this powerful feature.

### **Adding a Scheduling Block**

To add a scheduling block in CoachIQ, follow these steps:

1. **Navigate to the Scheduling Section**: Begin by accessing the scheduling section within your CoachIQ account.
2. **Edit the Block via the Sidebar**: Use the sidebar to locate and edit the scheduling block options.
3. **Click on "Add a Schedule"**: Initiate the scheduling process by selecting the option to add a new schedule.

### **Selecting a Session**

When selecting a session for your scheduling block, you have two options:

1. **Select from Sessions Built within CoachIQ**: Choose from pre-existing sessions that are already configured in CoachIQ.
2. **Create a Custom Session**: Alternatively, design a custom session tailored to your specific needs.

For this guide, we will choose a pre-built session.

### **Customizing the Scheduling Block**

#### **Session Selection**

- **Choose Your Session**: For instance, selecting the "shooting machine" session will automatically fill in all relevant information from that session.

#### **Button Functionality**

- **"Book Now" Button**: This button directs users to book the selected session.
- **"Learn More" Button**: Offers users an expanded description of the session, providing additional context and information.

#### **Additional Features**

1. **Media Addition**: Enhance your scheduling block by adding media, such as videos or photos, which is particularly useful for events like camps.
2. **Information Editing**: Customize various aspects of the session, including the title, location, and other pertinent details.
3. **Button Customization**: By default, the button links to the selected session, but you can modify the destination to suit your needs.

### **Use Cases**

This scheduling block feature is particularly beneficial for:

- Running camps and workshops
- Hosting sessions directly on your webpage
- Addressing a variety of scheduling requirements

### **Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Can I customize the media content in my scheduling block?**

A: Yes, you can add videos or photos to your scheduling block to enhance its appearance and provide additional information.

**Q: Is it possible to change the default button link?**

A: Absolutely. You can customize the button's destination to direct users to any URL you choose.
