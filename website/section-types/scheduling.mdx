**Where to find this:** website builder → **Add Section** → **Default Templates** → **Schedule**
</Info>

A Schedule section shows session cards, each with a title, length, price, location, description and **Book Now** and **Learn More** buttons. Pick one of your schedulers and the card fills in from it, so families can book from your website. Use it for camps, clinics, lessons or a free first session.

## Add the section

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Go to **Website & App** → **Website**, click **Edit website**, and pick the page from the page menu at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Schedule">
    Click **Add Section**, open **Default Templates**, click **Schedule**, then **Add to page**. The section lands at the bottom of the page; drag it where you want it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Click the section in the left panel. Under **Schedulers**, click **Add session** and pick what to show.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**, then check the live page on a computer and a phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a session to the section

Click **Add session**. **Select a Session Type** asks for one of two kinds:

- **CoachIQ Session**: opens **Schedulers**, a list of your schedulers with **Duration**, **Price** and **Location**. Search or sort it, then tick one. The card fills in from that scheduler, and **Book Now** opens its booking.
- **Custom Session**: a blank card you fill in yourself, for something you don't book through CoachIQ. Set where its buttons go with the gear.

Click **Add session** again to add more cards. The trash icon on a card removes it.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Subheading** | A short line above the heading, often in capitals. |
| **Heading** | The section's title. Up to 500 characters. |
| **Description** | A line or paragraph under the heading. |
| **Title** | The card's name. Click the pencil next to it to change it. |
| **Add Media** | Turn on to add a photo or video to the card. |
| **Date/Time** | The line next to the clock icon, such as "60 mins" or "June 9–12, 9am–noon". |
| **Price** | The price line, such as "\$40", "1 Credit" or "Free". |
| **Location** | The address under the title. |
| **Location Url** | Where the address links, usually a Google Maps link. |
| **Description** | A few lines about the session. |
| **Buttons** | **Book Now** and **Learn More**. Change the labels, or click the gear to send a button somewhere else. See [Buttons & CTAs](/website/sections/buttons-ctas). |

The words on the card are yours to edit. The booking itself, including available times, capacity, price and payment, always follows the scheduler. Change those in [Schedulers](/scheduling/creating-your-first-scheduler).

For **Background**, **Vertical Padding** and width, turn on **Change Section Styles**. See [Backgrounds & Styling](/website/sections/backgrounds-styling).

## What visitors see when they book

- **Time zone:** booking calendars show your schedule's time zone, for example "Times shown in PDT", and repeat it next to the list of times. It's always your schedule's zone, not the visitor's. A week where the clocks change reads PDT/PST.
- **Guest booking:** when a session allows guests (see [Guest checkout](/payments/guest-checkout)), **Reserve My Spot** offers **Continue as guest** alongside sign in. Guests enter a first and last name and don't need a password. If the session needs an account, visitors are asked to sign in or create one. A visitor who creates an account partway through goes straight to payment for the time they picked, with their details filled in.
- **Credits:** a credit booking stops at a confirmation window that shows the athlete's available credits and what the booking uses before anything is spent. If they don't have enough, the window says how many more they need and offers **Add more credits** when the session has a credit pack link set.
- **Access-code sessions:** the calendar shows the same open days as your app. If no days are open, for example because the athlete reached a booking limit, the calendar says why.
- **Archived sessions:** if a section still points at a session that was archived or deleted, the calendar says "This session is not available." Pick an active session for the section and publish.
- **Book Now** and **Learn More** buttons that continue to your app open it in a new tab and show a small new-tab icon. Members already signed in on your website arrive signed in.

The multi-step booking form template uses wording that fits both free and paid sessions ("Book a Session", "Upcoming Session"), and the session card shows the real price. Wording you saved yourself is kept.

## Tips

- After you publish, click **Book Now** on the live page and go as far as the payment step to check it opens the scheduler you meant.
- For a camp, write the dates in **Date/Time** instead of the session length, and add a photo from last year's camp with **Add Media**.
