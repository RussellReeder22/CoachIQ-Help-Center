**Where to find this:** website builder → **Add Section** → **Default Templates** → **Testimonials**
</Info>

A Testimonials section shows quotes from athletes and parents, each with a name and a title such as "Parent of a 12U player". Visitors slide through them, a few at a time.

## Add the section

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Go to **Website & App** → **Website**, click **Edit website**, and pick the page from the page menu at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Testimonials">
    Click **Add Section**, open **Default Templates**, click **Testimonials**, then **Add to page**. The section lands at the bottom of the page; drag it where you want it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Click the section in the left panel. The template comes with eight placeholder testimonials. Replace them with real ones and delete the ones you don't need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**, then check the live page on a computer and a phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Subheading** | A short line above the heading, often in capitals. |
| **Heading** | The section's title. Up to 500 characters. |
| **Description** | A line or paragraph under the heading. |
| **Testimonials** | The quotes, numbered **01**, **02** and so on. The trash icon deletes one. |
| **Name** | Who said it, for example "Maria R." |
| **Title** | Who they are to you, for example "Parent of a 14U pitcher" or "Varsity shortstop". |
| **Testimonial** | The quote itself. A long quote is cut short on the page with a **Read More** link. |
| **Add Media** | Turn on to add a photo to that testimonial, from **Upload New Media** or **Select from Media Hub**. You also get **Media Alt Text**, **Media Size** (**Contain** or **Cover**) and **Media Background**. |
| **Add testimonial** | Adds another quote at the end. |

For **Background**, **Vertical Padding** and width, turn on **Change Section Styles**. See [Backgrounds & Styling](/website/sections/backgrounds-styling).

## What visitors see

- The quotes slide, with dots under them to move between them. When every quote fits on screen, the arrows hide; they stop at the last quote.
- A photo you add to a testimonial shows in every layout. An optional background or video cover image can be taken off with **Remove**.
- You can start with one testimonial and add more as you collect them.

## Tips

- Ask parents for one specific result, such as "his exit velo went up 6 mph this winter". That reads as real; "great coach!" doesn't.
- Get permission before you use a family's name or photo, and use a first name and last initial for minors.
