<Info>
**Where to find this:** website builder → **Add Section** → **Default Templates** → **Credibility**
</Info>

A Credibility section shows three big numbers with a short line under each, such as "150+ athletes trained" or "12 years coaching". Parents comparing coaches look for this kind of proof, so it often sits near the top of the home page or right above pricing.

  ![A Credibility section with three numbers and a description under each](/images/credibiltiy-example.avif)
</Frame>

## Add the section

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Go to **Website & App** → **Website**, click **Edit website**, and pick the page from the page menu at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Credibility">
    Click **Add Section**, open **Default Templates**, click **Credibility**, then **Add to page**. The section lands at the bottom of the page; drag it where you want it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Click the section in the left panel and replace the three numbers and their descriptions with your own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**, then check the live page on a computer and a phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

The section always has three stats, **01**, **02** and **03**. You can't add a fourth or delete one.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Number** | The big figure, such as "150+", "12" or "30 D1". It's plain text, so you can add a plus sign or a word. |
| **Description** | The line under the number, such as "Athletes trained" or "Years coaching". |

There's no heading on this section. For **Background**, **Vertical Padding** and width, turn on **Change Section Styles**. See [Backgrounds & Styling](/website/sections/backgrounds-styling).

## Tips

- Use numbers a parent can check and cares about: athletes trained, years coaching, college commits, sessions run. Leave out vague claims.
- To show more than three numbers, add a second Credibility section right below the first.
