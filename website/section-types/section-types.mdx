Every page on your website is a stack of sections between the header and the footer. This page lists the standard sections and what each one is for. To add, reorder, hide or delete a section, see [Working with Sections](/website/sections/working-with-sections).

## Where sections come from

Click **Add Section** in the left panel of the builder. The library has three sources:

- **Published sections**: ready-made designs, including the Varsity and Momentum styles. Filter them with **Section types** such as Hero, Booking, Pricing or Testimonials.
- **Previous Pages**: sections already used somewhere on your site.
- **Default Templates**: the 11 standard CoachIQ sections in the table below.

## The default sections

| Section | What it's for | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Content + Media** | A photo or video next to a heading, text and buttons. Works as a hero at the top of a page or a call to action further down. | [Content & Media](/website/section-types/content-media) |
| **Form** | One of your CoachIQ forms, so families can register, sign up or pay on your site. | [Forms](/website/section-types/forms) |
| **Pricing** | Product cards with prices and a **Purchase** button that opens checkout in your app. | [Pricing](/website/section-types/pricing) |
| **Schedule** | Session cards with **Book Now** and **Learn More** buttons, filled in from your schedulers. | [Scheduling](/website/section-types/scheduling) |
| **Features** | A row of cards for what athletes get from training with you. Each card opens to show a short description. | [Features](/website/section-types/features) |
| **Numbered Steps** | Numbered cards that walk a family through how to get started. | [Numbered Steps](/website/section-types/numbered-steps) |
| **Credibility** | Three big numbers, such as athletes trained and years coaching. | [Credibility](/website/section-types/credibility) |
| **Testimonials** | Quotes from athletes and parents, with a name and title for each. | [Testimonials](/website/section-types/testimonials) |
| **CoachIQ Gallery** | A grid of photos and videos from your Media Hub, picked by tag. | [Gallery](/website/section-types/gallery) |
| **Blog Posts** | Your latest blog posts under a heading and description. | [Blog](/website/blog) |
| **Spacer** | Empty space between two sections. Under **Change Section Styles**, set how tall it is with **Vertical Padding** and pick its **Background**. | [Backgrounds & Styling](/website/sections/backgrounds-styling) |

## What every section has

Click a section in the left panel to open its settings. Most sections share these parts:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Hide** | Keeps the section on the page in the builder but hides it from visitors. |
| **Section name** | Click the pencil next to it to rename the section. Visitors don't see the name. |
| **Change Section Styles** | Shows **Background**, **Vertical Padding**, **Width** and **Max Width**. See [Backgrounds & Styling](/website/sections/backgrounds-styling). |
| **Subheading**, **Heading**, **Description** | The text at the top of the section. Each has a switch to show or hide it. The sliders icon next to the switch opens the advanced editor for that text. |
| **Buttons** | A label and a gear icon. The gear sets where the button goes. See [Buttons & CTAs](/website/sections/buttons-ctas). |

The fields below that depend on the section. Published sections can have their own fields, so they may look different from the default ones.

## Images and videos in sections

When a section crops a photo or video to fit a card, use its **Focus** setting to choose which part stays visible. Optional images, such as backgrounds and video covers, can be taken off with **Remove**; the file stays in your Media Hub. See [Working with Sections](/website/sections/working-with-sections#choose-which-part-of-a-photo-or-video-stays-visible).

## Maps

A map section shows your facility's location. Update its **Map source** with the map embed URL, publish, and check that the live map points to the right place. A map on your website doesn't change the location on your schedulers; manage those under [Locations](/scheduling/setup-locations).
