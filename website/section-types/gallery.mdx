A CoachIQ Gallery shows a grid of photos and videos from your [Media Hub](/content/media-overview). You don't pick files one by one: you tag them in Media Hub, enter the tag in the section, and every file with that tag shows.

<video controls className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" src="https://share.coachiq.io/nqRWXFx0" />

## Add the section

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Go to **Website & App** → **Website**, click **Edit website**, and pick the page from the page menu at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add CoachIQ Gallery">
    Click **Add Section**, open **Default Templates**, click **CoachIQ Gallery**, then **Add to page**. The section lands at the bottom of the page; drag it where you want it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Click the section in the left panel. Tag the files you want in Media Hub, then type that tag in **Search media tags...** and pick it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**, then check the live page on a computer and a phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose what shows

1. Click the pencil next to **CoachIQ Gallery** (**Upload/Edit Media**) to open your media. Upload new photos and videos or pick existing ones.
2. Give them a tag, for example "Homepage gallery". See [Organize with tags](/content/media-overview#organize-with-tags).
3. Close the media window, type the tag in **Search media tags...** and pick it. Search finds a tag from a letter or part of its name.

Every file with that tag shows in the gallery.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Subheading** | A short line above the heading, often in capitals. |
| **Heading** | The section's title. Up to 500 characters. |
| **Description** | A line or paragraph under the heading. |
| **Search media tags...** | The tag whose files the gallery shows. |
| **View Options** | **Default** or **Static Grid**. **Static Grid** shows every tagged item at once in a plain grid. |
| **Max Columns** | The most columns the gallery uses. |
| **Column/Row Gap** | The space between items. |
| **Row Height** | How tall each row is. **Default** view only. |
| **Border Radius** | How rounded the corners are. Set it to 0 for square corners. **Default** view only. |

For **Background**, **Vertical Padding** and width, turn on **Change Section Styles**. See [Backgrounds & Styling](/website/sections/backgrounds-styling).

## Galleries with individual items

Some published gallery sections, such as the auto-scrolling mixed media gallery and the masonry gallery, list each photo or video as its own item instead of using a media tag.

- **Add** creates a blank item. Use **Duplicate** when you want a copy of an existing one.
- An item you delete stays deleted after you publish, and a new item starts blank instead of bringing back an old caption or alt text.
- Under each photo or video, use **Focus** to choose which part stays visible in the card. See [Choose which part of a photo or video stays visible](/website/sections/working-with-sections#choose-which-part-of-a-photo-or-video-stays-visible).
- An optional video cover image can be taken off with **Remove**. The video itself stays.

## If the gallery is empty

- Check the tag in **Search media tags...** is the one on your files, and that the files are tagged in Media Hub.
- Changes show on your live site only after you **Publish**.

## Tips

- Use one tag per gallery, such as "Home gallery" or "Summer camp 2026", so tagging a file for one page doesn't add it to another.
- Pick photos where faces sit near the middle. Gallery tiles crop to fit, and centered subjects survive the crop.
