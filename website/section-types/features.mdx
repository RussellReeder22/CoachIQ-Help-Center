<Info>
**Where to find this:** website builder → **Add Section** → **Default Templates** → **Features**
</Info>

A Features section shows a row of cards for what athletes get from training with you, such as skill instruction, small-group sessions or your app. Each card shows a short title, and visitors click it to open a description.

<Frame>
  ![A Features section with a heading and a row of feature cards](/images/features-section-example.avif)
</Frame>

## Add the section

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Go to **Website & App** → **Website**, click **Edit website**, and pick the page from the page menu at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Features">
    Click **Add Section**, open **Default Templates**, click **Features**, then **Add to page**. The section lands at the bottom of the page; drag it where you want it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Click the section in the left panel. Write the heading, then rename each feature and write its description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**, then check the live page on a computer and a phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Subheading** | A short line above the heading, often in capitals. |
| **Heading** | The section's title. Up to 500 characters. |
| **Description** | A line or paragraph under the heading. |
| **Features** | The cards. Click the pencil on a feature to rename it (up to 350 characters) and fill in its **Description** (up to 750 characters). The trash icon deletes it. |
| **Add Feature** | Adds another card. |

There are no icons, images or buttons on these cards. For **Background**, **Vertical Padding** and width, turn on **Change Section Styles**. See [Backgrounds & Styling](/website/sections/backgrounds-styling).

## Tips

- Keep titles to a few words, like "Small-group sessions", so they fit on one or two lines. The description is where you explain it.
- Write about what the athlete gets, not how you run things: "Video review after every lesson" tells a parent more than "Advanced technology".
