## What SEO means for coaches

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is how parents and athletes find you when they search for training in your area. When someone types "basketball training near me" or "youth soccer coach [your city]," SEO determines whether they find you or your competitor.

<Note>
**The CoachIQ advantage**: Your website handles the technical SEO for you: sitemap, crawl rules, page tags, structured data and mobile-friendly pages. See the full list below.
</Note>

## What CoachIQ handles for you

Every CoachIQ website generates these automatically. You don't need to add code or plugins for any of them.

| What | Where to see it | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **XML sitemap** | `yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml` | Lists your visible pages for search engines. Updates when you publish. |
| **robots.txt** | `yourdomain.com/robots.txt` | Tells search engines which pages they may crawl. Pages you hide in the [Sitemap Builder](/website/seo-marketing/sitemap-builder) are blocked here. |
| **llms.txt** | `yourdomain.com/llms.txt` | Lists your visible pages in plain text for AI tools. |
| **Page title and description** | Each page's **Page Settings** | Become the title and snippet in search results. See [Meta Titles & Keywords](/website/seo-marketing/meta-titles-descriptions). |
| **Canonical tag** | Each page | Tells search engines the main address of the page, so duplicate addresses don't compete. |
| **Robots tag** | Each page | `index, follow` on visible pages and `noindex` on hidden ones. |
| **Structured data** | Each page | Describes your business (Organization), your site (WebSite) and the page (WebPage) in a format search engines read. |
| **Social share tags** | Each page | Control the title, description and picture shown when a page is shared. The picture comes from the page's **Featured Image**. See [Favicon & Share Images](/website/seo-marketing/favicon-share-images). |
| **Site name** | Search results, link previews | Built from your organization and group names. See [Organization & Group Name](/settings/change-organization-group-name#how-these-names-appear-on-your-website). |
| **Favicon sizes** | Browser tab, phone home screen | Generated from the favicon you upload. |
| **HTTPS and mobile-friendly pages** | Every page | Secure connection and layouts that fit phones. |


You focus on coaching and your content. CoachIQ handles the technical foundation.

## AI search and your website

More parents now ask AI tools such as Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Claude to recommend training near them. Those tools work from your **public website**, the same pages a search engine reads. They can't see anything inside your CoachIQ account.

That's different from connecting your own assistant to CoachIQ:

| | AI search | Your connected assistant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who uses it** | Parents and athletes looking for training | You and your staff |
| **What it reads** | Your public website pages | Your CoachIQ business, after you sign in and approve access |
| **Where to set it up** | The SEO guides on this page | [Use CoachIQ with AI](/getting-started/use-coachiq-with-ai) |

Connecting ChatGPT or Claude to your CoachIQ account doesn't make your website more visible in AI search, and AI search tools can't read your clients, schedule or payments.

### What helps

Google says the same SEO practices apply to its AI features. To be eligible, a page has to be indexed by Google and able to show in search results with a snippet.

- **Say what you offer on public pages.** Name your sport, your services (private lessons, small groups, camps, clinics), the age groups and skill levels you train, and the towns you serve. Put it in the page text, not only in images.
- **Give each page a clear title and description.** See [Meta Titles & Keywords](/website/seo-marketing/meta-titles-descriptions).
- **Keep your core pages visible** in the [Sitemap Builder](/website/seo-marketing/sitemap-builder). Hidden pages are blocked from search engines and left out of your `llms.txt`.
- **Add a redirect** when you rename or remove a page. See [URL Redirects](/website/seo-marketing/url-redirects).
- **Set up Google Search Console** and submit your sitemap. See [Google Search Console Setup](/website/seo-marketing/seo-google-search-console).

<Note>
  Your CoachIQ site already publishes an `llms.txt` file at `yourdomain.com/llms.txt`, listing your visible pages in plain text for AI tools. You don't need to add one, or special schema markup, or a separate "AI version" of your site. Google says files like `llms.txt` neither help nor hurt your visibility in its search results; other AI tools may use them.
</Note>

### What no one can guarantee

Getting indexed makes your pages eligible. It doesn't guarantee they'll appear in an AI answer. Google decides what to show for each search, and other AI tools use their own sources and don't publish how they choose. Be cautious of anyone who promises placement in AI results.

### How to check it

Measure AI search separately from regular search:

- **Google AI Overviews and AI Mode:** in Search Console, the generative AI performance reports show how often your pages appeared in Google's AI features, which pages, and in which countries. They report impressions, not clicks. The same data is also counted in the main Performance report, so don't add the two together.
- **Visits from other AI tools:** with [Google Analytics](/website/analytics-tracking/google-analytics) connected, some visits from tools like ChatGPT show up as referral traffic from that tool's site. Not every visit is labeled.
- **Mentions:** neither CoachIQ nor Search Console tracks whether an AI tool mentions your business. Once a month, ask a few tools the question a parent would ask, for example "softball pitching lessons for 12-year-olds in Plano." Answers change often, so treat it as a spot check.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to do anything for SEO?">
    Your site launches optimized. For best results, set up Google Business Profile, collect reviews, and keep your content updated. The guides above walk you through each step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long until I show up in Google?">
    Google decides when to crawl and list your site, so there's no fixed timeline. Submitting your sitemap in [Google Search Console](/website/seo-marketing/seo-google-search-console) helps Google find it. Ranking well for competitive searches usually takes months of steady effort, and your Google Business Profile tends to show up sooner than your website.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's most important for local coaches?">
    In order: (1) Google Business Profile, (2) Reviews, (3) Website content with location keywords.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
