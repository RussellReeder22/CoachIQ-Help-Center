## What SEO means for coaches

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is how parents and athletes find you when they search for training in your area. When someone types "basketball training near me" or "youth soccer coach [your city]," SEO determines whether they find you or your competitor.

</Note>

## AI search and your website

More parents now ask AI tools such as Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Claude to recommend training near them. Those tools work from your **public website**, the same pages a search engine reads. They can't see anything inside your CoachIQ account.

That's different from connecting your own assistant to CoachIQ:

| | AI search | Your connected assistant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who uses it** | Parents and athletes looking for training | You and your staff |
| **What it reads** | Your public website pages | Your CoachIQ business, after you sign in and approve access |
| **Where to set it up** | The SEO guides on this page | [Use CoachIQ with AI](/getting-started/use-coachiq-with-ai) |

Connecting ChatGPT or Claude to your CoachIQ account doesn't make your website more visible in AI search, and AI search tools can't read your clients, schedule or payments.

### What helps

Google says the same SEO practices apply to its AI features. To be eligible, a page has to be indexed by Google and able to show in search results with a snippet.

- **Say what you offer on public pages.** Name your sport, your services (private lessons, small groups, camps, clinics), the age groups and skill levels you train, and the towns you serve. Put it in the page text, not only in images.
- **Give each page a clear title and description.** See [Meta Titles & Keywords](/website/seo-marketing/meta-titles-descriptions).
- **Keep your core pages visible** in the [Sitemap Builder](/website/seo-marketing/sitemap-builder).
- **Add a redirect** when you rename or remove a page. See [URL Redirects](/website/seo-marketing/url-redirects).
- **Set up Google Search Console** and submit your sitemap. See [Google Search Console Setup](/website/seo-marketing/seo-google-search-console).

<Note>
  You don't need an llms.txt file, special schema markup or a separate "AI version" of your site. Google says these files neither help nor hurt your visibility in its search results.
</Note>

### What no one can guarantee

Getting indexed makes your pages eligible. It doesn't guarantee they'll appear in an AI answer. Google decides what to show for each search, and other AI tools use their own sources and don't publish how they choose. Be cautious of anyone who promises placement in AI results.

### How to check it

Measure AI search separately from regular search:

- **Google AI Overviews and AI Mode:** in Search Console, the generative AI performance reports show how often your pages appeared in Google's AI features, which pages, and in which countries. They report impressions, not clicks. The same data is also counted in the main Performance report, so don't add the two together.
- **Visits from other AI tools:** with [Google Analytics](/website/analytics-tracking/google-analytics) connected, some visits from tools like ChatGPT show up as referral traffic from that tool's site. Not every visit is labeled.
- **Mentions:** neither CoachIQ nor Search Console tracks whether an AI tool mentions your business. Once a month, ask a few tools the question a parent would ask, for example "softball pitching lessons for 12-year-olds in Plano." Answers change often, so treat it as a spot check.

## Common questions
