There are two ways to use AI with CoachIQ. Both answer from your own records, and both make changes only after you approve them.

| | The Assistant | ChatGPT or Claude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What it is** | A chat built into CoachIQ | Your own ChatGPT or Claude account, connected to CoachIQ |
| **Where you use it** | [Home](/home/overview), or **Tools → Assistant** in clubs without Home | In ChatGPT or Claude |
| **Setup** | None. It's already in your account | [Connect it](/getting-started/use-coachiq-with-ai) once, for one group |
| **Where you approve changes** | On a card in the CoachIQ chat | In ChatGPT or Claude, and a few removals in CoachIQ |
| **Good for** | Quick questions and one change at a time while you work in CoachIQ | Longer work in a Project that knows your business, and group email or text |
| **Start here** | [The Assistant](/tools/assistant/overview) | [Use CoachIQ with ChatGPT or Claude](/getting-started/use-coachiq-with-ai) |

The two don't have the same list of actions or the same limits. For example, ChatGPT and Claude can change only the text, images and links of your website's custom sections and a page's search details, while the Assistant can also change menus, pages and design. Each "what it can do" page says which one it covers:

- [What the Assistant can do](/tools/assistant/what-it-can-do)
- [What ChatGPT and Claude can do](/settings/ai-what-it-can-do)

## Rules both follow

- **You approve every change.** Each one shows what will change before anything saves, and an approval covers only that change. If the record changes in the meantime, the change is refused and nothing saves.
- **They work as you.** Each one reaches only what your role and [staff access](/people/staff-access) allow. A coach limited to certain clients only reaches those clients.
- **Bookings are free.** A booking made through AI takes no payment and uses no credits. Take payment or credits separately if the session should be paid for.
- **No money moves.** Neither one refunds, charges a card, or changes, pauses or cancels a subscription. See [Issuing refunds](/payments/issuing-refunds).
- **Automations are saved switched off.** You turn them on yourself in [Automations](/tools/automations/what-are-automations).
- **They never retry on their own.** If a change might or might not have saved, check the record in CoachIQ before asking again.

How approvals look is different in each one:

- [Assistant approvals, undo and limits](/tools/assistant/approvals)
- [ChatGPT and Claude approvals, activity and limits](/settings/ai-approvals-and-activity)
