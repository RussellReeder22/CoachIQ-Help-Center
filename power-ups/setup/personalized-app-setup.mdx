Once you've requested your Personalized App, this guide walks you through everything needed to get it built, approved, and live in the appropriate store. The detailed Apple enrollment steps below apply to iOS only. If you haven't requested one yet, start with the [Personalized App](/power-ups/personalized-app) overview.

<Info>

  **How it works**: Request iOS, Android, or both. CoachIQ coordinates the build and store submission with you. For iOS, set up an Apple Developer account and invite CoachIQ as described below. For Android, use the Android setup section.

</Info>

## Android setup

Android Personalized Apps are available. Request the Android version through **Power-Ups → Personalized App**, or contact the CoachIQ team if you already have an iOS app.

1. Tell the team whether you need Android only or both platforms.
2. Provide your business name, high-resolution logo, app icon, brand colors, and listing description.
3. Confirm the Google Play publishing-account and access requirements with the team before creating an account or granting access. Apple enrollment and Apple invitations do not configure Android.
4. Review the Android build with the team, then wait for confirmation that your Google Play listing is live before sharing its download link.

Store approval and release timing are separate for iOS and Android. Your existing iOS launch does not automatically publish an Android app.

## What You'll Need for iOS

Before getting started, have these ready:

- A credit card for the Apple Developer Program fee (99 USD/year, or local pricing shown by Apple)
- Your business's legal name and D-U-N-S number (required for Organization enrollment)
- Your coaching brand assets: high-resolution logo, brand colors (hex codes), app icon
- Access to your business email

## iOS setup process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request the Personalized App">
    In your CoachIQ admin, go to **Power-Ups** in the bottom-left sidebar. Find the **Personalized App** card and click **Request**.

    <Frame caption="Request the Personalized App from the Power-Ups page in your admin">
      ![CoachIQ admin Power-Ups page with Personalized App Request button highlighted](/images/power-up-request-app.png)
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      Have your brand colors (hex codes), high-resolution logo, and app store description ready before submitting your request.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your Apple Developer account">
    You'll need an Apple Developer account enrolled as an **Organization** (not Individual) to publish your branded app.

    ### Enrollment steps

    1. **Create a free Apple Developer account**: Go to [developer.apple.com](https://developer.apple.com/), click **Account**, and sign in with your Apple ID

    2. **Enroll in the program** ($99/year): Click **Join Apple Developer Program** → **Enroll** → **Start Enrollment**

    3. **Select Organization** as your entity type: Enter your company information and submit your application

    4. **Wait for Apple's approval**: Apple will verify your information and send an email with your enrollment status

    5. **Complete your enrollment**: After approval, log in to [Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/) and access [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/)

    <Warning>
      **Important**: Enable auto-renewal for your Apple Developer membership to prevent app interruptions. Complete any verification steps Apple requests after enrollment.
    </Warning>

    <Note>
      Need detailed guidance? Review Apple's official [Enrolling as an Organization](https://developer.apple.com/support/app-account/#organization) guide before starting.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite CoachIQ to your Apple Developer account">
    Grant CoachIQ the access needed to build and publish your app.

    ### How to send the invitation

    1. Sign in to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/) with your Apple ID

    2. Open **Users and Access**

    3. Click the **+** button to add a new user

    4. Enter the CoachIQ developer email: **[dev@coachiq.io](mailto:dev@coachiq.io)**

    5. Configure the following permissions:
       - **Role**: Admin
       - **Access to Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles**: ✓ Enabled

    6. Continue through **Next**, then click **Invite** to send the invitation. See Apple’s [user invitation instructions](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-your-team/add-and-edit-users) if the screen differs.

    <Check>
      **Invitation sent!** CoachIQ will receive an email from Apple and accept the invitation to begin building your app.
    </Check>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide your brand assets">
    Our team will reach out to collect:

    - Your logo (high-resolution)
    - Brand colors (hex codes if you have them)
    - Business name as you want it to appear
    - A brief description of your business

    We'll handle the rest, including designing your App Store screenshots and listing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next for iOS

After you complete the steps above:

1. **We build your app** — Our team creates your branded iOS app using your submitted assets
2. **You review the test version** — We'll push a test build directly to your device for approval
3. **App Store submission** — Once you approve, we submit your app to Apple for review
4. **Launch** — After Apple approves, your app goes live on the App Store

<Check>

  **Timeline**: The team will confirm your launch estimate. Account verification, your review, and each store’s approval can affect the date.

</Check>

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do I need an Organization account instead of Individual?">
    Organization accounts allow you to transfer app ownership, add team members, and present your business professionally in the App Store. Individual accounts are tied to a single person and have limitations for business use.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is a D-U-N-S number and how do I get one?">
    A D-U-N-S number is a unique nine-digit identifier for businesses issued by Dun & Bradstreet. Apple requires this for Organization enrollments. You can [request a free D-U-N-S number](https://developer.apple.com/support/D-U-N-S/) through Apple's lookup tool if you don't have one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I check if my invitation to CoachIQ is pending?">

    Go to **Users and Access** in [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/). If the status shows "Pending" next to [dev@coachiq.io](mailto:dev@coachiq.io), the invite hasn't been accepted yet. Allow 1–2 business days for our team to accept.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if my Apple Developer membership expires?">
    Your app will be removed from the App Store until you renew. Enable auto-renewal in your Apple Developer account to prevent interruptions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Apple rejected my Organization enrollment">
    Common reasons include: mismatched business name with D-U-N-S records, incomplete business registration, or missing verification documents. Contact [Apple Developer Support](https://developer.apple.com/contact/) for specific guidance on your rejection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't find the Users and Access section">

    Open [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/) and choose **Users and Access**. Make sure the Apple account has the permission needed to invite users.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The invitation email to CoachIQ bounced">
    Double-check you entered **[dev@coachiq.io](mailto:dev@coachiq.io)** exactly. If the issue persists, contact CoachIQ support and we'll provide an alternative email address.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
