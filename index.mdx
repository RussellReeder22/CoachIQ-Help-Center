Your account, Stripe, connections, notifications and imports are in [Settings](/settings/index). Services you can add, such as a branded app, are in [Power-Ups](/power-ups/index).

## A few words we use

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Clients** | Everyone in your People list: athletes, parents, coaches and admins. |
| **Scheduler** | The setup for one bookable service: availability, price, location, coaches and rules. |
| **Session** | One time on the calendar that an athlete books. |
| **Credits** | Session credits that a package or membership gives an athlete to book with. |
| **Your app** | What your athletes and parents use, on the web and on their phones. |
| **Messaging credits** | Your business's balance for texts and other metered messages. |

## Get help

Click **Help** at the bottom of the admin sidebar to ask Support, or [email us](mailto:info@coachiq.io) with the page you were on and what happened. You can also [join Office Hours](https://cal.com/team/coach-iq/office-hours) for a walkthrough with our team. The feedback buttons at the bottom of every article tell us which guides need work.
