<Info>
**Where to find this**: **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar. **General** is the first group in the Settings menu: **Account**, **Organization**, **Brand kit**, **Groups**, **Blocklist** and **Connections**.
</Info>

Every page in Settings saves the same way: change what you need, then click **Save** at the bottom. While you have changes that aren't saved, the bottom bar says **Unsaved changes**; **Reset** puts the page back to what was saved.

## Account

Your own profile. It follows you into every CoachIQ group you work in.

| Section | Fields |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | **Photo** (**Upload photo**), **First name**, **Last name** |
| **Contact** | **Email** (the address you sign in with, and where your alerts go), **Phone** (choose the country, then the number) |

Change a field and click **Save**.

<Tip>
Use a clear headshot. Your photo shows next to your messages in the Inbox and in the app your families use.
</Tip>

## Organization

Your business details. Only Admins can change them.

### Organization

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Logo** | Shows your current logo. Click **Edit in brand kit** to change it; logos and colors live in the [Brand kit](/website/brand-kit). |
| **Currency** | The currency for all your products and payments: USD, AUD, CAD, EUR, GBP or NZD. |
| **Name** | Your organization's name. |
| **URL** | The part of your admin address after `admin.coachiq.io/`. |
| **Domain** | Your own domain, if you use one. To connect a domain to your website, see [Domain setup](/website/domain-setup/domain-setup). |
| **Welcome URL** | Where new people are sent after they join. |

### Active group

The group you're working in now. If you run more than one group, switch first, then edit.

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The group's name. |
| **URL** | The group's part of your admin and app addresses. |
| **Reply-to email** | Where replies go when a client answers an email from CoachIQ. Leave it blank and replies go to your organization's main admin email, so they still reach you. |

<Warning>
Changing a **URL**, or your currency after you've built products, has knock-on effects. Read [Rename your organization or group](/settings/change-organization-group-name) before changing a URL. Set your currency before you create products.
</Warning>

## Groups

Every group you work in, so you can switch between them.

- **Current group** shows the group you're in now.
- **Staff groups** lists the other groups where you're staff, with your role in each. Click **Open** to switch to one. You can also switch from the card at the top of the sidebar; see [Switch between groups](/getting-started/find-your-way-around#switch-between-groups).
- **Leave** takes you out of a group. You lose access to it straight away.

<Note>
A group always keeps at least one Admin. If you're the last Admin, you can't leave, and nobody can remove or block you, until someone else is an Admin. Admins also can't change their own role or another Admin's role, or remove or block another Admin.
</Note>

If you also train as an athlete with another business on CoachIQ, that group is listed here too.

## Blocklist

Block someone by email so they lose access to your group straight away and can't join it again. You can block someone before they've ever signed up.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Blocklist">
Go to **Settings → Blocklist**. The list shows everyone blocked, with **Name**, **Blocked** (when) and **Reason**. Use the menu above the list to switch between **This group** and blocks that cover your whole organization.
</Step>
<Step title="Block someone">
Click **Block**, enter their **Email** and, if you want a record of why, a **Reason (optional)** (up to 500 characters). Click **Block**.
</Step>
<Step title="Unblock">
Use the actions on their row to unblock them.
</Step>
</Steps>

To block someone who is already a client, it's quicker from their profile. See [Block or remove a client](/people/block-and-remove-athletes), which also covers what a blocked person sees.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="How do I change my password?">
Log out, then choose **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. CoachIQ emails you a link to set a new one.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I work in more than one business?">
Yes. Each business adds you as staff, and every group you're in shows under **Settings → Groups** and in the switcher at the top of the sidebar.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why can't I edit Organization?">
Organization settings are for Admins. Ask an Admin in your business to make the change.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
