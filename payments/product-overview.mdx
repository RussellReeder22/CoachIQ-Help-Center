A **product** is anything families buy from you in CoachIQ, or get with a code you give them: a drop-in session, a 10-session package, a monthly membership, a camp registration, or program access. The product sets the price, how often it bills, and whether the purchase adds credits.

Open **Payments → Products** to see and create products.

## The three product types

| Type | How the family pays | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **One-Time** | One payment at checkout | Drop-in sessions, credit packages, camps, program access |
| **Subscription** | Recurring payment until it ends or is canceled | Memberships, monthly credit plans, ongoing program access |
| **Access Code** | Redeems a code you give them, with no card payment | Sponsored, scholarship, partner, or staff access |

You choose the type when you create the product. See [Product types](/payments/product-types) to choose between them.

<Note>
  Access codes unlock a product. They are not discounts. To reduce a price, use a [coupon](/payments/coupon-overview).
</Note>

## The Products page

The Products page lists your products with their price and creation date. Use the tabs to filter by type: **All Products**, **One-Time Payment**, **Subscriptions**, and **Access Codes**. Use the status filter to switch between **Active**, **Archived**, and **All**, and the search box to find a product by name.

<Frame>
  ![Products page with type tabs, status filter, and the row menu open showing View/Edit Product and Archive Product](/images/product-archive-menu.png)
</Frame>

Select a product to open it in the [Product Builder](/payments/product-builder). The row menu also offers **View/Edit Product** and **Archive Product**.

## Create a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new product">
    Go to **Payments → Products** and select **+ Create**. Choose **One-Time**, **Subscription**, or **Access Code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Add a name, a description, and any feature bullets. Families see these at checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the payment">
    Enter the price. For a subscription, set how often it bills. Add credits if the purchase should pay for sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select the create button at the top right of the builder, for example **Create Subscription**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A one-time or subscription product needs a name and a price. Enter **\$0.00** for a free product.

<Warning>
  [Connect Stripe](/payments/connecting-stripe) before creating paid products. Without a connected Stripe account, a product saves as a free, code-only product even if you enter a price.
</Warning>

The [Product Builder reference](/payments/product-builder) covers every setting.

## Where families find a product

<span id="product-visibility-where-athletes-can-find-products" />

Saving a product doesn't show it to anyone. Families can only buy it after you place it somewhere or share its link:

| Place | How to set it up |
| --- | --- |
| **App Products page** | Add the product to a section of your app's Products page. See [Products page](/athlete-portal/products). |
| **Direct link** | Copy the link from the product's **Product Link** section and send it. |
| **Scheduler** | Use it as the payment for a paid session. See [Use products in schedulers, programs, and forms](/payments/attach-products). |
| **Program** | Select it in the program's **Share** settings to sell access. |
| **Form** | Connect it under the form's **Connections**. |
| **Website** | Show it in a pricing or product section of your website. |

Because nothing is public until you place it, you can build products ahead of time and add them when you're ready to sell. There is no separate draft mode.

## Archive a product

Archive a product to stop selling it. Open the row menu on **Payments → Products** and select **Archive Product**, or select **Archive** at the top of the Product Builder.

When a product is archived:

- No one can complete a purchase, including through its direct link.
- Existing purchases, subscriptions, and credits stay as they are. Cancel a subscription separately if it should stop billing. See [Managing subscriptions](/payments/manage-subscriptions).
- Transaction history is kept.
- It isn't removed from pages, schedulers, or forms where you placed it. Remove it there so families don't see an offer they can't buy.

To find or restore an archived product, set the status filter to **Archived**, open the product, and unarchive it.

<Frame>
  ![Products page status filter showing Active, Archived, and All](/images/product-archive-filter-dropdown.png)
</Frame>

To hide a product you still want to sell to some people, remove it from the places families browse and share the link only with them. On a paywalled app page, use [hidden products](/athlete-portal/hidden-products-paywall).

## Good to know

- **Stripe:** Paid products are created in your connected Stripe account, so you will also see them in Stripe.
- **Edits apply going forward:** Changing a product affects new purchases. Existing transactions, subscriptions, and issued credits keep what they had when they were bought.
- **Fees:** Each product chooses whether the family pays a processing fee on top of the price. See [Processing fees](/payments/processing-fee).
- **Credits are optional:** Many products only take payment. Turn on credits when the purchase should pay for future sessions. See [Credits overview](/payments/credits/credit-system-overview).
