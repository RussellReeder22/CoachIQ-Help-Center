Open **Payments → Products**, then select **+ Create** for a new product or select an existing product to edit it.

The builder lists its sections on the left. The **Product Preview** on the right summarizes the product as you go, including its processing fee, frequency, credits, credit limits, and confirmation page. Save with the button at the top right, for example **Create Subscription** or **Update One Time**. **Archive** sits beside it on an existing product.

## Details

- **Product Name** (required): what families see at checkout and in their purchase history. Name it after what it unlocks, such as "Group Training - 8 Credits a Month", not "Package A".
- **Product Description**: what's included, who it's for, and any expiration or restriction.
- **Add Feature**: short bullet points shown with the product.

## Payment

<span id="section-2-payment-options" />

**Price** is required for One-Time and Subscription products. Enter **\$0.00** for a free product. Access Code products don't need a price.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Add processing fee">
    Turn on **Add processing fee** to charge the buyer a fee on top of the price. Set a **Fixed Fee** and a **Percentage Fee**. The **Preview** below the fields shows the price, fee, and total. Leave it off to absorb the fees yourself.

    You can also set the label buyers see for the fee. Fee amounts, the CoachIQ platform fee, and ACH fees are covered in [Processing fees](/payments/processing-fee).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Enable access codes">
    Turn on **Enable access codes** to let families redeem a code instead of paying. Add each code and how many times it can be used. Remove a code to stop new redemptions; purchases already made with it are unaffected.

    On an Access Code product, codes are the only way to get the product. On a One-Time or Subscription product, families can pay or enter a code. See [Product types: Access Code](/payments/product-types#access-code).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Payment methods at checkout">
    Products don't choose their own payment methods. Buyers see the methods that are active for your organization: cards, plus Apple Pay and Google Pay, US bank account (ACH), and buy-now-pay-later options such as Afterpay where enabled. ACH works on one-time products and recurring memberships. See [Payment methods at checkout](/payments/getting-started#payment-methods-at-checkout).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Show payment methods (collect a card without charging)">
    **\$0.00 products only.** Turn on **Show payment methods** to have the buyer enter a card at checkout without being charged. The card is saved to their profile. Use it to get cards on file before a season starts.

    Saving a card doesn't schedule a charge. To charge automatically on a future date, use a subscription with **Schedule charges for later**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Add an initial down payment (subscriptions)">
    Adds a one-time amount to the first payment only, such as an enrollment or registration fee. Later payments charge the regular price.

    Example: a \$50 down payment on a \$200-a-month membership charges \$250 at signup, then \$200 a month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Subscription length (subscriptions)">
    - **Ongoing** (default): bills until the family or you cancel.
    - **End after a number of payments**: for example, 4 payments for a season billed monthly.
    - **End on a date**: for example, the last day of the season.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Schedule charges for later (subscriptions)">
    The family checks out now and saves a payment method, and the first charge runs automatically on the **First charge date**. Use it to open registration early and bill everyone when the season starts.

    Families see **Billing starts [date]** rather than a trial.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Enable free trial (subscriptions)">
    Set the **Trial period (days)**. The family isn't charged until the trial ends and can cancel before then. The client's [Subscriptions tab](/people/client-profiles/client-profiles-subscriptions) shows **Trialing** during the trial.

    A product can use a free trial or **Schedule charges for later**, not both.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  **Older one-time products:** free trials and **Schedule charges for later** are subscription-only. If an older one-time product still has one of them on, the builder shows **Only available for subscriptions** and won't save until you turn it off. Paid checkout for that product is blocked until then. To bill on a future date, create a subscription instead.
</Note>

<span id="understand-the-amounts-clients-see" />

### The amounts families see

The **initial payment** is what the family pays at signup, including any down payment and coupon. The recurring amount is what later payments charge. A coupon that applies to the first payment only doesn't reduce later payments.

Example: a \$15-a-month membership with a \$5 down payment and a 50% coupon for two months has a \$10 initial payment before fees, then \$7.50 a month while the coupon lasts, then \$15 a month.

To give members an automatic discount on other products, create a member discount under **Payments → Coupons**. See [Member discounts](/payments/coupon-overview#member-discounts).

## Frequency

**Subscriptions only.** Set **Payment Every** to a number and a period: Day, Week, Month, or Year. For example, every 1 month, every 3 months, or every 1 year. If the product adds credits, they are added on the same schedule.

## Credits

<span id="section-3-credits" />

Credits turn a product into a package or membership that pays for sessions. The product decides how many credits a family gets and when they expire. Each scheduler decides how many credits a booking costs. Read this with [Credits and schedulers](/scheduling/credits-and-schedulers).

| You want to… | Set it on the | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set how many credits a purchase adds | Product | **Credits → Number of Credits** |
| Set when credits expire | Product | **Credits** expiration options |
| Choose which schedulers accept the credits | Product | **Credit Limits** |
| Cap credits per scheduler each billing cycle | Product | **Credit Limits → Limit credits per scheduler per billing cycle** |
| Make a session cost credits | Scheduler | **Payment → Paywalled → Credit** or **Either** |
| Set how many credits a booking costs | Scheduler | **Payment** credit cost |
| Return credits on cancellation | Scheduler | Credit refund setting. See [Cancel a booking or session](/scheduling/canceling-bookings). |

<Warning>
  A product that adds credits needs at least one scheduler set to **Paywalled** with **Credit** or **Either**, and allowed under **Credit Limits**. Otherwise families buy credits they can't spend.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Add Credits once Purchased">
    **Off** (default): the product only takes payment.

    **On**: each purchase adds credits to the account the purchase belongs to. For a subscription, credits are added every billing period. See [who the purchase is for](#who-the-purchase-is-for).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Number of Credits">
    How many credits each purchase, or each billing period, adds. Match it to your schedulers' credit costs: 10 credits cover 10 one-credit sessions or 5 two-credit sessions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Credit expiration: one-time products">
    - **Days after purchase**, for example 90 days
    - **A specific date**, such as the end of a season
    - **Never expire**
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Credit expiration: subscriptions">
    - **Unused Credits Expire upon Subscription Frequency**: leftover credits are removed when the next period starts.
    - **Carry over unused credits**: leftover credits roll into the next period.
    - **Unlimited Credits**: families see **∞** instead of a credit count. See [Credits overview](/payments/credits/credit-system-overview) for how unlimited credits work.

    When a subscription ends, credits from its final period end with it under the expire option.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

State the expiration in the product description so families know before they buy. Some places regulate how soon prepaid credits can expire, so check local rules before setting a short expiration.

<span id="require-sessions-to-happen-before-credits-expire" />

### Require sessions to happen before credits expire

Turn on **Sessions must occur by expiration** when a credit should only pay for sessions held before it expires. It works with expiration by date, days after purchase, or subscription period.

- **On:** the session date must fall on or before the credit's expiration date. A reschedule must stay inside that window. A recurring booking needs an end date, and every occurrence must fit.
- **Off:** the credit only needs to be valid when the booking is made. The session can take place later.

For a membership period of September 1–28, turning this on lets those credits pay for a September 28 session but not an October 2 session, even if the family books in September. The booking calendar still shows later dates; the credit just can't pay for them.

A credit returned after a cancellation keeps its original expiration. See [Recurring credit memberships](/payments/credits/recurring-credit-memberships).

## Credit Limits

Shown when credits are on.

- **Credits can be Redeemed for these Schedulers**: choose the schedulers that accept this product's credits. Leave it empty to allow every credit scheduler. When you pick schedulers, the credits only work there, and other schedulers tell the family they have no usable credits.
- **Limit credits per scheduler per billing cycle**: for subscriptions, cap how many of the product's credits can be used on each selected scheduler in a billing period. For example, 4 on group training and 4 on private lessons from an 8-credit membership.

Restricting schedulers is the most common reason for "I have credits but can't book." Name the product after what it unlocks. The order CoachIQ checks restrictions, balances, and limits is in [Credit usage limits and restrictions](/payments/credits/credit-usage-limits-restrictions#limit-priority-hierarchy).

### Credit changes after families have bought

| Change | Effect on existing buyers |
| --- | --- |
| **Number of Credits** | None. Future purchases and renewals get the new amount. Add credits to existing buyers from each client's **Credits** tab. |
| **Expiration** | None. Credits already added keep their expiration. |
| **Credit Limits schedulers** | Immediate. Adding a scheduler lets every holder book there; removing one blocks them. Tell families before you remove one. |
| **Archiving the product** | None. Holders keep and can spend their credits. Update any scheduler whose **Buy more credits** link points to it. |
| **Refunding the payment** | Credits aren't removed automatically. Remove them from the client's **Credits** tab. See [Turning off a product's credits](/people/client-profiles/client-profiles-credits#turning-off-a-products-credits). |
| **A failed subscription payment** | New period credits stop. Existing bookings aren't canceled. See [Recurring credit memberships](/payments/credits/recurring-credit-memberships#payment-failure-involuntary-churn). |

A coupon lowers the price but doesn't change the number of credits.

## Confirmation page

Choose where the buyer goes after paying:

- **Direct users to your athlete portal** (default)
- **Direct users to a thank you page**
- **Direct users to a customer URL**: enter the full address, such as a page on your website. Test the link before you share the product.

## Product link

Every saved product has a direct checkout link. Copy it from the builder to send in a message, add to your website, or turn into a QR code. Anyone with the link can buy the product unless it's archived.

## Advanced

<span id="section-6-advanced-settings" />

<Frame>
  ![Advanced section with purchase limits, guest checkout, revenue sharing, Require Agreement to Terms with a selected waiver, and Allow users to cancel](/images/waivers-product-terms.png)
</Frame>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Purchase limits">
    - **Add a purchase limit for this product**: the total number of purchases across your business. When it's reached, the product is sold out. Use it for camp spots or roster sizes.
    - **Add a purchase limit for this product per user**: how many times one person can buy it. Set 1 for an intro offer.

    Example: a total limit of 24 and a per-user limit of 1 gives a 24-athlete camp where no family can register the same child twice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Allow users to checkout as guest">
    Lets someone buy without signing in first. Not available when the purchase is for a **Managed Athlete**. See [Guest checkout](/payments/guest-checkout).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Share revenue with another Stripe Account">
    Sends the full payment for this product to another CoachIQ organization's Stripe account instead of yours. It doesn't split payments by percentage. Contact support to set it up. See [Revenue sharing](/payments/sharing-rev).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Require Agreement to Terms">
    Requires the buyer to accept one or more waivers at checkout. Select an existing waiver, choose **+ Create new waiver**, or open **Manage all waivers**. Waivers live under **Tools → Waivers**, which also tracks who has accepted. See [Waivers](/tools/waivers/waivers).

    Older products may still use a single agreement link instead of a waiver.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Allow users to cancel">
    **Subscriptions.** Lets families cancel the subscription themselves from their account. Leave it off if cancellations should go through you. See [Managing subscriptions](/payments/manage-subscriptions).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Attach a registration form to a product

In **Advanced**, choose **Add a form**, select a form, and save the product. The form needs at least one question.

Checkout then goes **registration → review → purchase**, and required answers must be completed first. A parent or athlete who already completed registration for that product skips the form on a repeat purchase.

You can also start from the form and connect the product from its **Connections** menu. See [Forms with payments and bookings](/tools/forms/payments-and-bookings).

<span id="who-the-purchase-is-for-buyer-or-managed-athlete" />

### Who the purchase is for

At the bottom of the builder, choose who receives the purchase and its credits.

- **Buyer** (default): the purchase and credits belong to the account that pays. A parent buying for children holds one shared family balance. See [Booking for multiple athletes](/scheduling/booking-for-multiple-athletes).
- **Managed Athlete**: checkout asks **Who are you registering?** The parent picks a child, and the purchase, credits, and any tags go to that child. Use it for team registrations, seasonal programs, and per-athlete credits.

An adult athlete with no linked children is both buyer and athlete, so checkout doesn't ask.

With **Managed Athlete**, two registrations by one parent stay attached to the right child, so rosters, credits, refunds, and automations follow each athlete. Parents add children in the app under **Settings → Family**. See [Family accounts](/athlete-portal/family-accounts).

## Archive a product

Select **Archive** at the top of the builder. An archived product can't be bought, but existing purchases, subscriptions, and credits are unaffected. It isn't removed from your app, schedulers, or forms, so remove it there too. See [Archive a product](/payments/product-overview#archive-a-product).
