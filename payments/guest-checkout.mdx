Guest checkout lets someone pay, or book a free session, without signing in or creating a password first. Use it for a first purchase from an ad, your website, or a link you share.

The guest still gets a CoachIQ profile under the email they enter. They just don't need a password to check out. They can set one later on the sign-in screen with the same email.

## Where guest checkout applies

| What they're buying | What controls guest checkout |
| --- | --- |
| A product, from its link, your app, or your website | The product's **Allow users to checkout as guest** setting |
| A paid session (**Paywalled → Price**) | The same product setting on the product used for payment |
| A free session | The scheduler's own guest checkout setting |
| A session paid with credits | Not available. Credits are used from a signed-in account. |

## Turn on guest checkout for a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the product">
    Go to **Payments → Products** and open the product, or select **+ Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the setting">
    In **Advanced**, turn on **Allow users to checkout as guest**. The builder confirms that users won't need to sign in to purchase this product.

    <Frame>
      ![Advanced section with Allow users to checkout as guest turned on](/images/guest-checkout-setting.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the product" />
</Steps>

The setting doesn't apply when:

- The product's purchase is for a **Managed Athlete**. The parent signs in so they can choose which child they're registering.
- A subscription requires a registration form under **Advanced → Add a form**. The buyer is asked to sign in or sign up. A one-time product with a registration form can still use guest checkout.

## Turn on guest booking for a free scheduler

<span id="guest-checkout-for-schedulers" />

Use this for a free intro call, evaluation, or trial session. Open the scheduler under **Scheduling → Schedulers**, turn on its guest checkout setting, and save. Anyone with the scheduler's link can then book without signing in.

The scheduler setting only affects free sessions. A paid session follows the product's setting, and a credit session always requires sign-in.

## What the guest enters

- Name and email
- Payment details, for a paid purchase
- Answers to any form connected to the product or scheduler

## What you see

- The purchase appears in **Payments → Transactions** like any other.
- A guest booking appears on your calendar with the guest's name, so you can check in, move, or cancel it as usual.
- CoachIQ keeps a profile under the email the guest used, so later purchases and bookings stay together.

## When to use it

**Good fits**

- Free intro calls, evaluations, and trial sessions
- Paid drop-ins and one-off clinics bought from a link or ad
- Event and camp registrations
- Merchandise or one-time fees

**Keep it off for**

- Credit packages and memberships: families need to sign in to book with credits and manage their membership.
- Program access: content is viewed in the signed-in app.
- Team or family registrations where you need to know which child is attending. Use **Managed Athlete** instead.

<Tip>
  For a sales funnel, turn on guest checkout for the first step, such as an intro session, and keep it off for the packages and memberships that come after. Once someone is a client, have them sign in so they can see bookings, use credits, and get reminders in the app.
</Tip>
