## What guest checkout is

Guest checkout lets people pay with just their contact and payment details - no account creation required. It exists in two places:

- **Products** - a toggle on each product, for merchandise, event fees, and other one-off sales.
- **Schedulers** - a toggle on each scheduler, so someone can book an intro call or trial session straight from an ad, a quiz funnel, or your website without hitting a login wall.

**With guest checkout enabled:**

- Guest enters name and email
- Guest enters payment details
- Purchase or booking completes
- No password or portal login is created

**With guest checkout disabled (default):**

- Person must create a CoachIQ account (or log in)
- Account required before checkout
- Person gets Athlete Portal access

## When to enable guest checkout

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Enable for">
    **Sales and bookings that don't need ongoing access:**

    ✅ **Merchandise or equipment sales**

    - T-shirts, gear, supplements
    - One-time physical products
    - No need for portal access

    ✅ **One-time workshops or events**

    - Single-session clinics
    - Drop-in classes
    - Public seminars

    ✅ **Intro calls and trial sessions (schedulers)**

    - The first touch in a marketing funnel
    - Free or paid trial sessions
    - Anyone landing from an ad or your website can book immediately

    ✅ **Non-client purchases**

    - Community members
    - One-time visitors
    - People who won't book again

    ✅ **Gift purchases**

    - Someone buying for another person
    - No need for buyer to have account

    <Tip>
      Enable guest checkout when you're selling to the general public, not just your established client base. For a funnel, put guest checkout on the intro-call scheduler and keep it off everything that comes after.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Keep disabled for">
    **Products and schedulers that need an account:**

    ❌ **Subscriptions or memberships**

    - Ongoing access to portal
    - Credit tracking over time
    - Recurring billing management

    ❌ **Program access**

    - Need account to view content
    - Videos, documents, workouts
    - Progress tracking

    ❌ **Multi-session packages**

    - Credits require account
    - Must book sessions over time
    - Need to track usage

    ❌ **Recurring or credit-based schedulers**

    - Credits can only be spent from an account
    - Rescheduling and cancellation happen in the portal
    - Reminders and history need a profile

    <Warning>
      If a product issues credits or grants program access, guest checkout won't work properly. Guests can't use credits or access portal content.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Enable guest checkout on a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Product Builder">
    Go to **Payments → Products** and either:

    - Click **\+ Create Product** for new product
    - Click existing product to edit
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Advanced Settings">
    Scroll to **Section 6: Advanced Settings**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle guest checkout ON">
    Find **Guest Checkout** toggle and switch to ON.

    <Frame>
      ![Guest checkout toggle in CoachIQ Product Builder advanced settings](/images/guest-checkout-setting.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save product">
    Click **Create.**

    <Check>
      **Guest checkout enabled.** People can purchase without creating an account.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Guest checkout for schedulers

Schedulers have their own guest checkout setting, separate from any product. Turn it on and anyone who reaches the scheduler - from your booking page, your website, or a link in an ad - can reserve a time without logging in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the scheduler">
    Go to **Schedule → Schedulers** and open the scheduler you want to open up (typically an intro call or trial session).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on guest checkout">
    In the scheduler builder, switch the guest checkout setting ON and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the scheduler link">
    Use the scheduler's link or embed it on your website. Visitors book without being asked to create an account.

    <Check>
      **Guest booking enabled.** The login step is skipped for this scheduler only.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What a guest provides when booking:**

- Name and email (and phone, if the scheduler asks for it)
- Payment details, if the scheduler is paid
- Any form connected to the scheduler is still completed

**What you see:**

- The booking appears on your calendar with the guest's name and contact details
- The guest is not added to your client list and has no portal login
- For a paid scheduler, the payment appears in **Payments → Transactions** like any other

<Note>
  Guest bookings are best for the first session. Once someone becomes a client, have them create an account so they can reschedule, use credits, and receive reminders in the portal. Booking schedulers only accept guests when this setting is on; a scheduler with guest checkout off still requires login even if the attached product allows guests.
</Note>

Scheduler payment settings (free, price, or credits) are covered in [Setting up payment options](/scheduling/setting-up-payment-options).

## What guests provide at checkout

**Required information:**

- Email address
- Name
- Payment method (a card, or any other payment method you've enabled - see [Payment methods at checkout](/payments/getting-started#payment-methods-at-checkout))

**NOT required:**

- Password
- Phone Number (unless the scheduler or form asks for it)
- Account creation
- Profile setup

<Info>
  Minimal friction checkout. Faster for one-time buyers who don't need ongoing access.
</Info>

## What guests receive

**After purchase or booking:**

✅ **Email receipt**

- Payment confirmation
- Purchase details
- Transaction amount

✅ **Product, booking, or access (if applicable)**

- Booking confirmation for a guest-enabled scheduler
- Event confirmation if workshop/clinic

❌ **No Athlete Portal access**

- Can't log into CoachIQ
- Can't view purchase history
- Can't manage account or reschedule from the portal

❌ **No credits**

- Even if product issues credits
- Credits require account to track and use

❌ **No booking on other schedulers**

- Only schedulers with guest checkout ON can be booked as a guest
- Everything else still asks for an account

## What you see

**In your Transactions:**

- Guest purchases appear normally
- Transaction recorded with email
- Payment processed successfully

**In your client list:**

- Guest does NOT appear as client
- No profile created
- Email captured but not as full client record

**On your calendar (scheduler guests):**

- The booking shows with the guest's name and email
- Treat it like any other booking for check-in, cancel, or reschedule

<Note>
  Guest transactions are tracked for your records, but the guest doesn't become a full client in your system.
</Note>

## Guest checkout flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Guest selects product or time">
    Guest finds the product on your booking page or website, or picks a time on a guest-enabled scheduler.

    Clicks purchase, buy, or book.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Guest enters name & email">
    Checkout form asks for name and email address.

    No "create password" field appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Guest enters payment">
    Guest provides payment details (skipped for a free scheduler).

    Standard Stripe payment form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Purchase or booking completes">
    Transaction processes immediately.

    Guest receives email receipt or booking confirmation.

    <Check>
      **Complete.** Guest paid or booked, you received the order, no account created
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Guest vs. account checkout comparison

| Feature              | Guest Checkout                                   | Account Checkout        |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------- |
| **Speed**            | Faster (contact + payment)                       | Slower (create account) |
| **Portal access**    | No                                               | Yes                     |
| **Purchase history** | No                                               | Yes                     |
| **Session booking**  | Yes, on schedulers with guest checkout ON        | Yes                     |
| **Reschedule / cancel from portal** | No                                | Yes                     |
| **Credits**          | No                                               | Yes                     |
| **Subscriptions**    | No                                               | Yes                     |
| **Programs**         | No                                               | Yes                     |
| **Best for**         | One-time sales, intro calls, trial sessions      | Ongoing services        |
