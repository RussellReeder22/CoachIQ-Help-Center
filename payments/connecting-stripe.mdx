Open **Settings → Stripe**, or **Settings → Connections → Stripe → Manage**.

## Connect your account

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Stripe settings as an Admin">

Choose the correct CoachIQ organization. Only Admins can connect or disconnect Stripe; Coach-role staff see guidance to ask an administrator.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose Connect Stripe">

Sign in to the Stripe account you want to use, or create one through Stripe's flow. If you have several accounts, check the business before authorizing.

</Step>

<Step title="Authorize and return to CoachIQ">

Complete the Stripe flow and return to the integration page. Check the business name, masked account ID, and live/test label.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Check account health

<Frame>

![Stripe test account showing separate connection, payment, and payout status](/images/stripe-connection-health-2026-09.png)

</Frame>

This example shows a **Test account**. Check your own account label before accepting live payments.


The account card separates **Connected to CoachIQ**, **Payments enabled/paused**, and **Payouts enabled/paused**. A connected account is not necessarily ready to take payments or receive payouts.

If Stripe needs more information, use **Review in Stripe** and follow its requirements. A verification notice does not mean you should reconnect. If account status is unavailable, retry instead of treating the account as healthy or disconnected.

**Open Stripe dashboard** opens the connected account. The live/test label identifies the connected environment; switching a view in Stripe's dashboard does not switch CoachIQ's payment environment.

## Understand product relationships

**Products in this Stripe account** shows Stripe products, pricing, and their relationship to CoachIQ. Use Previous/Next for longer lists.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| In CoachIQ | The Stripe product has a saved relationship to at least one CoachIQ product. Use **Open in CoachIQ** to view it. |
| Stripe only | The product has not been added to CoachIQ. This is informational, not a failed payment or import request. |
| Status unavailable | CoachIQ could not confirm the relationship. Retry rather than assuming it is unlinked. |
| Archived | The Stripe product is archived; an existing relationship does not make it purchasable. |

Connecting Stripe does not automatically add all Stripe products to CoachIQ. Use **Manage CoachIQ products** to open **Payments → Products** for product setup, prices, credits, and access. Matching is based on saved Stripe identifiers, not matching product names.

The old Products/Invoices tabs and import button in Stripe settings have been replaced by this view. Manage invoices in the appropriate billing area or Stripe dashboard.

## Disconnect

**Disconnect Stripe** is at the bottom of the page and requires confirmation. Disconnecting does not cancel existing Stripe subscriptions or delete your CoachIQ product setup. If the attempt fails, the confirmation shows the error with **Try again** and **Keep connected**; the connection remains intact. See [Disconnect Stripe](/payments/disconnect-stripe).

Demo Stripe connections managed by CoachIQ cannot be disconnected here.
