Give your assistant three things: **CoachIQ's documentation**, **a connection to your business**, and **context about your goals**. Together, they help it give more useful answers and carry out supported work in CoachIQ.

| What you provide | What it gives your assistant |
| --- | --- |
| Help Center | Instructions for how CoachIQ features work. |
| CoachIQ MCP connection | Current information and supported tools for your authorized business group. |
| Private Project | Your business brief, priorities, and working preferences across future chats. |

## 1. Connect your business

Choose the guide for your assistant. It walks you through adding CoachIQ, signing in, and selecting your group and access.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Connect to ChatGPT" icon="plug" href="/settings/connect-chatgpt">
    Connect CoachIQ, confirm access, and use it in your ChatGPT workflow.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Connect to Claude" icon="plug" href="/settings/connect-claude">
    Add the CoachIQ connector, authorize it, and enable it in a conversation.
  </Card>

**Already connected?** Ask your assistant:

> Check my CoachIQ connection with a small live read. Confirm my business, group, and whether you can read only or make supported changes. Don't change anything.

Continue once it confirms the intended business and a successful live read. Signing in and approving access happen through the connection flow; pasting a prompt or the Help Center URL does not complete those steps.

## 2. Give it CoachIQ's feature knowledge

Point your assistant to [help.coachiq.io](https://help.coachiq.io) and the [AI-readable guide index](https://help.coachiq.io/llms.txt). The index lists the available articles so it can choose the relevant guides instead of guessing how a feature works.

For a specific question, give it the article's URL. A plain-text Markdown version is also available by adding **.md** to an article URL, for example [the Sheets overview as Markdown](https://help.coachiq.io/tools/sheets/overview.md).

The [complete documentation text](https://help.coachiq.io/llms-full.txt) is available when your tool needs it, but it is large. Start with the index and relevant articles. If your assistant cannot open links, provide the relevant article text or file using its supported attachment controls. Saved copies need refreshing when the product changes.

<Note>
  Help Center access explains features; it does not grant access to your account. MCP access is limited to the tools and permissions actually available on your connection. A feature described here may still require you to use the CoachIQ app.
</Note>

## 3. Paste one Project setup prompt

Once the connection is confirmed, paste this into the connected chat. It combines the documentation, live business context, and your goals into a reusable setup.

```text
I'm a CoachIQ customer. Help me set up a private Project that understands my business and how I work.

First, use my connected CoachIQ MCP for a small live read. Confirm my business, group, and available access. If it isn't connected, guide me through the appropriate guide at https://help.coachiq.io/getting-started/use-coachiq-with-ai before continuing.

Read https://help.coachiq.io/llms.txt to find the relevant CoachIQ guides, then read those pages to understand how the features work. Use the available MCP tools to learn about my services, coaches, locations, scheduling, and products. Keep this focused; don't pull my entire client list or message history. Tell me if you can't access a source.

Ask up to three questions about my goals and working preferences. Then prepare a concise Business Brief, five useful requests tailored to me, and ready-to-paste Project Instructions.

The instructions should use the Help Center for feature guidance and the MCP for current business facts and supported actions. Stay within the connected group, ask before making changes or sending messages, and verify results. Keep private client details out of saved context. Only read data during this setup.

Suggest a Project name and help me save the brief and instructions. If you can save them directly, do so; otherwise give me the finished content and exact steps. Finish by checking that a new Project chat can use both the saved context and the live CoachIQ connection.
```

Answer its questions and follow the saving steps. Your assistant can prepare the content, but you may need to create the Project and save its instructions and files yourself. The [ChatGPT guide](/settings/connect-chatgpt#save-your-project) and [Claude guide](/settings/connect-claude#save-your-project) explain where to save them.

## 4. Check a new Project chat

Ask the new chat to summarize your priorities from the saved brief and perform a small live CoachIQ read. It should be able to identify both the saved context and the current business group. If the connector is unavailable in that chat or mode, return to the platform guide's troubleshooting steps.

Start with one of the five requests from your Business Brief. Keep the brief current when your services, goals, or working preferences change; use live data for details that change day to day.

## What your connected assistant can and can't do

A read-only connection can look things up. Changes need a connection with write access, and you approve each one in your assistant before it runs. The assistant works within your own role and [staff access](/people/managing-staff#managing-staff-access-blocking-pages) in the connected group.

### It can read

- **Clients:** a client's full profile, including contact details, tags, custom fields, waiver status, family links, credit balances and history, transactions, subscriptions and staff notes.
- **Products and payments:** products, coupons, transactions (including failed payments and pending bank payments) and subscriptions.
- **Your business setup:** scheduling, locations, programs, lessons, media and other records covered by its tools. Ask it to list its current tools before planning a task.

### With write access, it can

- **Update clients:** correct a client's first name, last name, phone number and custom fields; create Athletes and Parents; add and delete staff notes on a client's profile (currently up to 4,096 characters per note).
- **Manage tags:** add or remove a tag for up to 100 people in one change, and switch off a tag. Each result lists who was changed, who already had the tag and who was skipped.
- **Set up products and coupons:** create and edit products, attach them to forms and programs, set a product active or inactive, and create or remove coupons.
- **Manage credits:** set a client's credit balance, turn a client's credit use for a product on or off, add or remove a credit pack as a top-up option on a session that already charges credits, and add or remove the sessions where a pack's credits can be spent.
- **Organize media:** import, rename and tag files and attach them to programs and lessons. See [AI workflows](/settings/ai-workflows#find-and-organize-media).
- **Report a problem:** send a problem report to CoachIQ when something goes wrong, and check its status later.

### It can't

- Refund, charge a card, retry a failed payment, change or cancel a subscription, or change Stripe settings.
- Change anyone's role (including between Athlete and Parent), remove or block a person, invite staff, change a login email, or change sign-up field settings.
- Read message threads or saved payment methods.
- Edit an existing coupon, set one client's credit expiry, or attach a paid product to a scheduler.
- Make a credit change that would quietly affect every session, such as removing the last session from a pack's list. Set the full list in the product builder instead.
- Delete Media Hub files, change their visibility, or run Video Analysis.

For anything it can't do, ask for the steps and make the change in CoachIQ.

### Approvals and the activity log

Your assistant asks before each change. In Claude, the approval card opens with a plain-English summary of what will change. For a set of related changes, such as a tag clean-up, ask the assistant to plan the whole set first and choose **Allow for this task** once instead of approving every step.

Every change is logged under the assistant and the coach who approved it. In **Settings → Data → Logs**, changes made through ChatGPT or Claude carry an **Outside app** label, and requests CoachIQ refused carry a red **Refused** label. If your role or staff access changes, the assistant is told nothing changed and what to do next, which may be to reconnect.
