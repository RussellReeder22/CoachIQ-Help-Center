<Info>
**Where to find this**: **Settings → Data → Shared calendar**. Only an organization owner can change it.
</Info>

If you run more than one group under the same organization out of the same facility — for example a baseball program and a softball program sharing one building — a booking in one group won't automatically stop the same time slot from being booked in the other. Shared Calendar links two groups' calendars so they share busy time.

## How it works

Once two groups are linked, busy time is shared in both directions. Unlinking restores independent calendars for both groups.

When you link groups, choose how much busy time to share:

- **All busy time** — every booking in the linked group blocks the same time range on your calendar (and vice versa).
- **Resources only** — both groups share one resource list. Add each court, cage, field, or room once, and a booking in either group blocks that resource for the other. Busy time that has no resource attached stays private, and athlete names from the other group are never shown.

Shared Calendar does not merge staff lists or give you a single combined schedule screen. It only shares busy time between the two groups.

If the page says "No eligible groups are available to link", your organization has only one group, or no other group can be linked yet.

## Linking two groups

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Shared Calendar settings">
    As an organization owner, go to **Settings → Data → Shared calendar**. The **Linked groups** section lists the groups you can link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the groups">
    Select the other group in your organization. Choose **All busy time** or **Resources only** based on what should block bookings, then confirm the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unlink if needed">
    Return to the same settings page at any time to unlink the two groups and restore independent calendars.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you see on the calendar

The other group's bookings show on your **Scheduling → Calendar** as **Busy** blocks. They name the space in use, never the other group's athletes, coaches or session titles.

- **All sessions** shows Busy blocks. **My schedule** and a coach filter hide them so your own sessions use the full width. Use **Linked groups** in the filters to show or hide each linked group; your choice is saved with the view. Overlapping blocks show as one.
- Hiding Busy blocks never frees the time. It still can't be booked.
- Click a Busy block to see a small box with the group's name, the space and the time. It has no booking buttons. This works in Day, Week, Month and the Day view by Resources.
- If you coach in both groups, your own sessions in the other group show as real sessions (title, time, space and number of athletes, with a chip in that group's color). They have no actions here; manage them from the other group.
- The CoachIQ app's calendar works the same way, with a **Linked groups** section in **Filters**.

## Booking a space that's held

When you **Book session** and pick a scheduler, a date and a time, a **Space** list shows each of the scheduler's spaces as **Free** or **Held**. A space held by the linked group names only that group. A note says which free space the booking will take, or asks you to pick another time when none is free.
