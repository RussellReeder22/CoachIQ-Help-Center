## Offer more than one session length

Under **Advanced settings → Session lengths**, select the lengths families can book. They choose a length before a date and time. Changing the selected length clears the earlier time selection and refreshes availability.

Set overrides under **Payment → Price by session length**. A length without an override uses the default product or credit cost. See [Price by session length](/scheduling/pricing-by-session-length).

## Preview a specific date

In **Specific Dates**, the table shows each connected date's hours, session length, and resources. Use **All**, **Upcoming**, or **Past** to narrow the list.

Select a date to move the preview to that day. If it has no open times, the preview keeps your selection and shows **No bookable times on this date**. Check its hours, blocks, and booking rules.

Choose **Open availability** on the row to open the connected template on that date in a new tab, keeping your unsaved scheduler work in place. Date-level **Session length** and **Resources** each have their own **Save** action.

## A different duration on one date

Use this when a specific practice needs to run longer or shorter than usual:

1. Open **Specific Dates** on the scheduler.
2. Select a configured date from the connected availability template.
3. Enter **Session length** in minutes and choose its **Save** action.
4. Review the date in the preview. Use **Restore default** to remove the override.
5. Choose **Update** for any other scheduler changes before leaving.

If no specific dates are connected, add them to the availability template first. An explicit duration override must fit that date's window. Shared windows without an override follow the [shared-availability rules](/scheduling/create-your-availability#shared-availability-and-different-session-lengths).

Resources can also be overridden here. A per-date location is set on the availability date; see [Locations](/scheduling/setup-locations#a-different-venue-for-each-date).

## Active, inactive, and archived

Use **Active** to make an offering available for booking. Publishing it in the portal or website is a separate step. Turning it inactive pauses new athlete bookings without removing the configuration.

[Archive](/scheduling/organizing-schedulers#archiving-a-scheduler) a finished offering to remove it from the active catalog while keeping history. Neither pausing nor archiving replaces the work of canceling sessions already booked.

## Preview before publishing a change

Check the relevant date and length in **Preview**, save, then inspect the shared booking page. Changing a scheduler's setup is different from changing the duration, location, or participants of one booked session.

Use [Changing your schedule](/scheduling/changing-your-schedule) to choose the correct scope and [Share and publish](/scheduling/sharing-schedulers) to update where families find it.
