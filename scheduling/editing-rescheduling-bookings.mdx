## Editing a booking

On **Scheduling** → **Calendar**, click a session. The click view shows **Duration**, **Time Slot**, **Location**, **Coaches**, resources, and participants.

**Duration**, **Location**, and **Coaches** each have **Edit**. **Time Slot** does not; to change the date or time, use **Move Session** in the header (below). You can also add a coach or a resource to this one occurrence from the click view without opening the scheduler. Footer: **Add Participants** and **Send Message to All**.

<Frame>
  ![Calendar session click view with Duration, Time Slot, Location, and Coaches](/images/scheduling-calendar-click-duration.png)
</Frame>

## Edit duration from the calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Edit next to Duration">
    On the session click view, **Duration** shows the current length (for example **60 min**). Click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Duration">
    That opens **Edit Duration**. The field is **Duration in minutes**.

    Helper: the end time is named, and the change does not change the session date or start time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Duration or Cancel">
    **Notify participants** starts off. Turn it on only if you want to notify booked athletes, choose the available channels, and review the message. Click **Save Duration** to save, or **Cancel** to leave it unchanged.

    <Frame>
      ![Edit Duration with Notify participants switched off](/images/release-guides-sep30/edit-duration.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change a session location

From the calendar session details, choose **Edit** beside **Location**, then select the new location. If the location changes and athletes are booked, review **Notify participants** before choosing **Save Location**.

The dialog shows the booked-athlete count and a suggested message. Choose **Announcement**, **SMS**, **In-app message**, or **Email**, or turn notification off. The change and its selected notifications happen only after a successful save. Choosing the same location or editing a session with no bookings saves without that notification step.

<Frame caption="Test session showing notification choices before saving a new location.">
  ![Change Location with recipient count, channel choices, message, and Save Location](/images/release-guides-sep30/change-location.png)
</Frame>

## Which edits send messages

| Change in the admin calendar | Notification choice |
| --- | --- |
| Location of a booked session | Review Notify participants and the chosen channels before saving |
| Duration | Notify participants starts off |
| Move Session | Notify participants is available; In-app is preselected, with Email also available |
| One athlete's booking | **Email [athlete] about this change** starts unchecked |
| Resources | No athlete notification from the resource change |

<Note>
  Your own **New Booking** automations can still run when a booking is rescheduled. Turning off this dialog's notifications does not disable those automations. These steps describe the admin web calendar; do not assume the mobile app or a connected tool shows the same notification dialog.
</Note>

## Move an entire session

Use **Move Session** when the whole occurrence has to change date or time: a coach is sick, the facility is closed, a practice shifts from Tuesday to Wednesday. It moves the occurrence with everything attached to it in one action, instead of rebooking each athlete.

Where to find this: **Scheduling → Calendar → click the session**

### What moves with the session

- Every participant booking on that occurrence
- The assigned coach or coaches
- The attached resources

Nothing about the scheduler changes. Other dates of the same session stay where they are, and the moved occurrence keeps its location, price, and payment status. Review the proposed start and end times before confirming.

### Move the session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the session">
    Go to **Scheduling** → **Calendar** and click the session you want to move. **Move Session** is in the header of the click view, next to the red trash icon that opens [Cancel Session](/scheduling/canceling-bookings#cancel-an-entire-session-from-the-calendar).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Move Session">
    The **Move Session** dialog opens with the current date and time shown.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set New Date & Time">
    Pick the new date and review the start and end times under **New Date & Time**. Confirm that the resulting duration is what you intend.

    Moving a session does not check the new time against your availability template, so confirm the coach and any resources are actually free before you move it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose how to notify participants">
    **Notify participants** is on by default, with **In-app** pre-checked. Review the channels, including the optional **Email** summary for athletes and their families, or turn notification off. The message is prefilled with the original and new date/time; edit it to add a personal note.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Move Session**. The occurrence and all its bookings appear at the new time immediately; the old time is freed.

    <Check>
    After a successful save, notifications follow the channels you selected. Turning Notify participants off skips this step’s notifications.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![Move Session with Notify participants, In-app message, and Email options](/images/release-guides-sep30/move-session.png)
</Frame>

### Moving one athlete, not the whole session

**Move Session** moves every participant in that occurrence. To change one athlete's reservation, open that athlete's booking and use **Edit** to review its rescheduling options. The **Email [athlete] about this change** choice starts unchecked. If the new service or length requires a different payment, follow [canceling](/scheduling/canceling-bookings) and [rebooking](/scheduling/manually-booking-athletes) with the correct payment or credit handling. For one date of a recurring booking, use **Change** in the [Occurrences view](/scheduling/recurring-bookings#changing-one-occurrence) instead.

## Drag-and-drop rescheduling

For quick rescheduling directly on the calendar:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to Week or Day view">
    Use the view toggles at the top of your calendar to switch to **Week** or **Day** view.

    <Frame>
      ![Calendar week view showing multiple sessions](/images/calendar-week-view.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Drag the booking">
    Click and hold the booking you want to move, then drag it to the new time slot.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Release to confirm">
    Drop the session on the desired time, review any warnings or confirmation, and verify the saved result. Treat this as a change to the occurrence, including its participants; use the individual booking editor when only one athlete is moving.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Drag-and-drop rescheduling bypasses availability checks. Make sure the new time works for both the coach and athlete before moving.
</Warning>

## What athletes see

Booking confirmations can include a rescheduling link when athlete self-rescheduling is enabled. Check your notification settings and the booking recipient when troubleshooting delivery:

<Frame caption="The email athletes receive when a session is booked. The 'Re-schedule Here' link lets them move the session themselves when self-rescheduling is enabled.">
  ![Session confirmation email titled 'Your Session is Coming Up' showing date, time, location, description, and a re-schedule link](/images/session-confirmation-email.png)
</Frame>

When you **Move Session**, the announcement includes the original date and time and the new one by default. You can edit it to add a personal note. **Notify participants** is available, with **In-app** pre-checked.

The announcement follows this pattern:

`[session name] moved from [day, date] at [start - end] to [day, date] at [start - end].`

<Frame>
  ![In-app announcement after a move, naming the original time and the new time](/images/inbox-reschedule-moved-from-to.png)
</Frame>

When you move a booking:

- **Selected notifications** — When enabled, communicate the original and new details through the channels you chose
- **Portal update** — The updated session appears in their Athlete Portal immediately
- **Calendar sync** — Check the connected Google calendar if you use [Google Calendar sync](/scheduling/google-calendar-sync)

<Tip>
**Best practice**: If making a significant time change, send a personal message through the Inbox to confirm the athlete can make the new time.
</Tip>

## Controlling whether athletes can reschedule

You can configure athlete self-rescheduling on a per-scheduler basis in **Scheduler Settings → Booking Rules**. There are two related toggles:

**Allow athletes to reschedule** — When on, athletes can move their booking to a different available time slot from the Athlete Portal or app. An eligible reschedule retains its existing payment or credit arrangement; it does not collect a price difference. A differently priced length may require canceling and rebooking. Credit-paid sessions must still meet applicable expiration and session-date restrictions. When off, only coaches and admins can reschedule — athletes must contact you to make changes.

**Block rescheduling within cancellation window** — When on, athletes cannot reschedule once they're inside your cancellation notice window. This prevents athletes from using reschedule as a workaround to avoid forfeiting a credit on a late cancellation. The blocking window matches your Cancellation Policy (e.g., if you require 24-hour notice, rescheduling is also blocked within that same 24 hours).

[Configure rescheduling permissions →](/scheduling/scheduler-settings-limits-and-permissions#rescheduling-permissions)

## Warnings when moving a whole session

An athlete with another booking at the new time produces an amber heads-up. You can still move the whole session after reviewing it. Two sessions at the same saved location do not conflict merely because they share that location.

Red warnings still block the move, including an occupied resource or another occurrence of the same session at the proposed time. Review the named conflicting session or resource and choose a different time or resource. All warnings appear together, with blockers above heads-up messages.
