Open a scheduler under **Scheduling → Schedulers → Advanced settings**. These rules apply to that scheduler. [Defaults](/scheduling/setup-defaults) supply starting values for new schedulers; changing defaults does not update existing ones.

## Capacity and booking limits

| Control | Scope | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Max athletes per time slot** | Athletes attending the same occurrence | 1 for a private lesson; 6 for a group class |
| **Max total bookings per day** | All athletes and times on this scheduler | Accept up to 10 bookings in a day |
| **Max total bookings per week** | All athletes on this scheduler in its weekly limit period | Limit total use of this offering |
| **Max bookings per athlete per day** | One athlete across this scheduler's times | Each athlete can book twice daily |
| **Max bookings per athlete per week** | One athlete on this scheduler in its weekly limit period | Each athlete can book five times weekly |

The weekly scheduler limits use a rolling seven-day period. They differ from product billing-cycle credit restrictions. A booking must satisfy each applicable limit.

With a total daily limit of 10 and an athlete limit of 2, one athlete reaches their limit after two bookings even if the scheduler has capacity left. The tenth total booking uses the scheduler-wide allowance for that day.

Leaving **Max athletes per time slot** unchecked allows unlimited capacity. Preview then shows the number booked, rather than a one-athlete cap.

A group slot stays open until its capacity or another rule stops booking. Staff actions can show override confirmations; read the specific warning rather than assuming every limit can be bypassed.

## Timing and booking windows

| Control | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Duration / Session lengths** | How long an occurrence lasts or which lengths families can choose |
| **New sessions can start** | Spacing between offered starts |
| **Buffer before / after** | Time around a session used for preparation, cleanup, or travel |
| **Minimum notice** | How close to the start time an athlete can book |
| **Book up to / future booking window** | How far ahead self-booking is offered |

Shortening the start interval does not shorten sessions or automatically allow overlap. See [Start times and overlap](/scheduling/overlapping-slots). Preview the result before extending hours or reducing buffers.

## Cancellation and credit refunds

Two settings answer different questions:

- **Allow athletes to cancel** and the cancellation notice determine whether the athlete can cancel themselves.
- **Refund credits used on cancelation** determines whether an eligible self-cancellation returns redeemed credits.

| Situation | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Athlete cancels outside the notice window, refunds on | Redeemed credit is returned |
| Athlete cancels outside the notice window, refunds off | Redeemed credit is not returned |
| Athlete is inside the cancellation notice window | Self-cancellation is blocked; they contact staff |
| Staff cancels a credit booking | Review the refund choice in the cancel dialog |

The credit refund setting does not create a tiered refund policy or issue a card refund. See [Cancel a booking or session](/scheduling/canceling-bookings).

## Rescheduling permissions

**Allow athletes to reschedule** lets athletes move an eligible booking to another available time. **Block rescheduling within cancellation window** separately prevents moves inside the cancellation notice window.

A reschedule does not automatically collect a price difference. A different session length must satisfy the [length-pricing rules](/scheduling/pricing-by-session-length#existing-bookings-and-rescheduling), and credit-paid sessions remain subject to applicable credit restrictions.

## Repeat bookings and participant visibility

**Allow athletes to book a timeslot more than once** controls repeat reservations for the same athlete/account in that slot. It is separate from total capacity and per-athlete daily or weekly limits. For linked children, follow [Family booking](/scheduling/booking-for-multiple-athletes); do not replace child selection with repeated bookings under the parent's name.

**Show booked athletes to other athletes** controls whether other athletes can see who is booked. Decide whether participant names are appropriate for this offering.

**Team visibility** is a different setting: it controls which athletes may access the scheduler. See [Catalog, tags, and visibility](/scheduling/organizing-schedulers).

Use the booking preview and a controlled test athlete to check the rules you changed. Changing scheduler settings does not automatically cancel existing bookings or correct their payment records.
