## How scheduling fits together

| Part | What it does | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scheduler** | Defines the service, available lengths, payment, capacity, and booking rules | Hitting lessons with 30- and 60-minute options |
| **Availability** | Supplies the days and hours the scheduler can use | Mondays and Wednesdays, 3–7 PM |
| **Session** | A particular occurrence on the schedule | Wednesday at 4 PM |
| **Booking** | Reserves a place for an athlete in that occurrence | Alex attending the 4 PM lesson |

A group session can have several athlete bookings. One booking does not close the time unless capacity or another scheduling rule prevents further bookings.

Availability alone does not reserve a coach or a cage. Attach the appropriate [coaches](/scheduling/setup-coaches) and [resources](/scheduling/resources), then review their conflict settings. Duration, start times, buffers, booking windows, and existing reservations all affect which times can be booked.

| **Setup** | Manage reusable availability, coaches, locations, resources, and defaults for new schedulers |

## Decide when to use another scheduler

Use one scheduler for different lengths of the same service when the rest of the setup is shared. [Price by session length](/scheduling/pricing-by-session-length) lets a 30-minute lesson and a 60-minute lesson have different prices or credit costs.

Create a separate scheduler when the offering needs different capacity, booking rules, availability, or service details—for example, a private lesson and a group clinic. A different venue on one date can use a [per-date location](/scheduling/setup-locations#a-different-venue-for-each-date).

## Choose how people book and pay

Athletes and parents can book through your published schedule or a direct link. Staff can [book manually](/scheduling/manually-booking-athletes) or create a [recurring schedule](/scheduling/recurring-bookings). Parents select the attending child; see [family booking](/scheduling/booking-for-multiple-athletes).

A scheduler can be **Free** or **Paywalled**. Paid options are **Price**, **Credit**, or **Either**. Configure the payment option separately from the booking's date and time; creating a staff booking is not proof that a card payment was collected.

For a team season, start with [Teams practices and games](/people/teams/practices-and-games) when Teams is enabled for your organization. Team membership, payment, and a successful session booking are separate results.

## Make the scheduler available to families

Creating an active scheduler, placing it in the portal or website, and deciding who can book are separate steps. Follow [Share and publish schedulers](/scheduling/sharing-schedulers), then check [Team visibility](/scheduling/organizing-schedulers#team-visibility) if only part of your group should have access.

Before changing an established schedule, check [what happens to existing bookings](/scheduling/changing-your-schedule).
