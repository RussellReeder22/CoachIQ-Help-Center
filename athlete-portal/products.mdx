<Info>
  **Where to find this:** **Website & App › App**, click **Edit app**, then **Products** in the builder's left menu. The page is titled **Products page** in the builder.
</Info>

The Products page is your app's store: the memberships, credit packs, camps and other products families can buy. You create products in **Payments › Products** (see [Products overview](/payments/product-overview)); this page decides which of them show in your app and under which headings.

<Note>
  A new product isn't on this page until you add it. Families can also reach a product from a scheduler, a form, a message or its direct link. See [Where families find a product](/payments/product-overview#product-visibility-where-athletes-can-find-products). Your Stripe account must be connected before you can add products.
</Note>

## Add products to the page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Products page">
    In the builder, click **Products** in the left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Products">
    Inside a section, click **+ Add Products**. The **Products** window lists your products with their amount and the date they were created. Search by name, or filter by **Type**, status (**Active**) and order (**Recent**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick products">
    Select one or more products, then click **Add Selected**. Each one shows as a row with a title box you can change for this page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update** at the top right. Nothing changes in your app until you do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sections

Sections are the headings families see in the store, such as "Memberships" or "Camps".

- **Add a section:** click **+ Add section** under the last section, then type its name in **Section Title**.
- **Reorder:** drag a section or a product row by its handle (the six dots, named "Reorder" plus the name for screen readers). Rows only move from the handle, so the title and description boxes stay easy to click.
- **Remove:** click the trash icon. For a product it's named "Remove", the product name, "from this page". This only takes it off the page; the product itself isn't archived or changed.

## Turn the page on or off

The **Active** switch at the top turns the page on or off for families. You can also show or hide it, and change its address, in [Menu & Links](/athlete-portal/menu-and-links). Turning on the products that unlock paywalled pages is done in [Pages Paywall](/athlete-portal/paywall-settings).

<span id="configuring-page-settings" />

## Page settings

Click the gear icon (**Page settings**) at the top right. It has the same fields as the other pages:

- **Page name** (the first box): what families see as the page title and in the menu, for example "Shop". Leave it blank to use **Products**.
- **Slug:** the end of the page's web address, for example `/products`. The buttons beside it copy the link and open the page.
- **Page Meta (SEO):** the image, title, keywords and description that show when someone shares a link to the page.

Click **Update** to save.

## What families see

- **Product list and details:** each product shows its price and a **Purchase Now** button, in the list and in the product's details window. **Read More** shows only when the description is cut short, and long product names wrap on phones instead of being cut off.
- **A product link that no longer works** says the product isn't available, with a way back to your products.
- **Signing in:** a visitor who isn't signed in sees **Log in / Sign up** as the first checkout step.
- **Checkout, Billing and Orders:** what families see when they pay, and where they manage memberships, cards and credits afterwards, is in [Bookings, billing and orders](/athlete-portal/bookings-billing-orders#checkout).

## In the mobile app

The iPhone and Android apps show the Products page too. Depending on the family's app version and the product, they pay inside the app or the app opens checkout in the browser. See [Your app on iPhone and Android](/athlete-portal/native-app-booking).

If you have your own branded app on iPhone, the Products page comes with its next App Store version. Until then, families on that app can buy the same products in your app on the web.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A product isn't in the Products window. Why?">
    Check that it exists in **Payments › Products** and isn't archived, and that Stripe is connected. Archived products can't be added, and a direct link to one says the product isn't available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A family can't find a product in the app. What should I check?">
    1. The Products page's **Active** switch is on and you clicked **Update**.
    2. Products isn't hidden in [Menu & Links](/athlete-portal/menu-and-links).
    3. The product is on this page, in a section, and isn't archived.
    4. On a phone, their app is up to date. If their app doesn't offer the purchase, send them the product's link to buy on the web.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do credits bought on the web work in the mobile app?">
    Yes. Credits are on the person's account, so they can book with them on the web or in the app.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
