<Info>
**Where to find this**: Athlete Portal → **Settings** → **Family**
</Info>

Parents no longer need you to set up each child's booking access by hand. From their own account, they can add a family member and switch between managing each athlete right from the portal.

## What parents can do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    From the athlete portal, the parent selects **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Family">
    They select **Family**, where they'll see the athlete they're currently managing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a family member">
    Selecting **Add Athlete** lets them add another child to the account. They can also select an existing athlete listed there to switch to managing them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Booking for a specific family member

Once family members are added, booking a session includes a **Select Athlete** step. The parent picks which athlete the session is for — themselves or any linked family member — before checkout.

<Frame>
  ![Select Athlete step in the booking flow, showing Family Credits available and a list of athletes to book for](/images/booking-select-athlete-family-credits.png)
</Frame>

That step also shows **Family Credits available** — a single credit balance shared across every athlete on the account, with 1 credit deducted per session booked, no matter which family member the session is for. The booking record itself now shows which athlete it's for, so you no longer have to rely on notes, tags, or asking at check-in to tell family bookings apart — see [Booking for Multiple Athletes](/scheduling/booking-for-multiple-athletes).
