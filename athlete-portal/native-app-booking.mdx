Families can use your app on their phone as well as on the web. They download either:

- **The CoachIQ app** from the App Store or Google Play, if you don't have your own listing, or
- **Your branded app**, your own listing with your name and icon. See [Branded app](/power-ups/personalized-app).

The **App Store** and **Google Play** buttons on **Website & App › App** link to the right one for your business. Families sign in with the same account they use on the web, and see the same pages, names and design you set in the App builder.

<Note>
  Your coaches and staff use the CoachIQ app to run your business from their phone, not your branded app. A coach who signs in to the branded app sees what families see.
</Note>

## Booking a session

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a scheduler and a time">
    The athlete opens your Schedule page in the app and picks a scheduler and a time, the same as on the web. A session card's **Learn More** shows its description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who the session is for">
    A parent with more than one athlete picks which one the booking is for. See [Family accounts](/athlete-portal/family-accounts#booking-for-a-specific-family-member).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay or use a credit">
    It depends on how the scheduler is paid for:

    - **Credits only:** the app uses the credit and confirms the booking.
    - **Payment only:** the family pays at checkout. Depending on their app version, checkout happens in the app or opens the browser.
    - **Payment or credits:** the app asks whether to pay or use a credit, the same choice as on the web.

    An athlete whose credits are paused, for example after a failed membership payment, is offered payment instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find it under Bookings">
    The session shows in **Bookings** with its date, time and location. If you set a different venue for one date, that date's venue is what shows. A parent sees every athlete's bookings, grouped by athlete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Buying in the app or on the web

Whether a family can pay inside the mobile app depends on the version they have installed and on the product. When the app can't take the payment, it opens checkout in the browser: the family pays there and comes back to the app. They don't need to start again.

If a family can't find something to buy in the app, they can always buy it on the web from your app's address, and it shows in the mobile app straight away.

## When the phone app looks different from the web

Changes you make in the App builder, and new purchases and bookings, show in the mobile app straight away. Changes to the app itself, its screens and buttons, reach families when they install an update from the App Store or Google Play. Your branded app gets its updates separately from the CoachIQ app, so it can be a version behind for a while.

If a family sees something different on their phone than on the web:

1. Ask them to close and reopen the app.
2. Ask them to update the app from the App Store or Google Play.
3. If it's still wrong, ask them to try the same thing on the web, then contact support with what they see on each.

## Staying signed in

If CoachIQ is updated while someone is using the app, they're signed back in when it finishes. Anyone who still ends up logged out should update the app and sign in once.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="An athlete says the app sent them to the website to book">
    That's the payment path: the scheduler needs payment, or the athlete has no usable credit, so the app opened the browser for checkout. Check the athlete's **Credits** on their profile and that the scheduler accepts credits. If both look right, make sure they have the latest app version.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the location differ between two dates of the same session?">
    You set a different venue for that date. Each date can have its own location, and the app shows the one for that date. See [Locations](/scheduling/setup-locations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can athletes request account deletion in the app?">
    Yes. The request arrives in your **Inbox**; nothing is deleted automatically. See [Your app: overview](/athlete-portal/understanding-your-athlete-portal#common-questions).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
