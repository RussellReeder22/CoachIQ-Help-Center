This is the order that works for most facilities. Each step takes a few minutes to an hour and links to the full guide. Do them in order: later steps use what you built earlier (a scheduler needs a product, a product needs Stripe).

If you're new to the admin, read [Find your way around](/getting-started/find-your-way-around) first. It shows the sidebar and what's in each section.

<Tip>
  You don't have to do this alone. Bring your questions to [Office Hours](https://cal.com/team/coach-iq/office-hours), or ask Support from **Help** at the bottom of the sidebar.
</Tip>

## 1. Your business basics

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Stripe">
    Go to **Settings → Stripe** and click **Connect Stripe**. Until you do, products save as free and families can't pay you. Check that the account card shows payments and payouts enabled.

    [Connect Stripe](/payments/connecting-stripe)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your logo and colors">
    Go to **Settings → General → Brand kit**. Your website, your app and your emails all use it.

    [Brand Kit](/website/brand-kit)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your locations and spaces">
    Go to **Scheduling → Setup**. Add each address on the **Locations** tab. If you rent out or book cages, courts or rooms, add each one on the **Resources** tab so two sessions can't take the same space.

    [Locations](/scheduling/setup-locations) · [Resources](/scheduling/resources)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your coaches">
    Go to **People → Staff** and click **Add**. Then decide what coaches can see with **Default access**, and limit any coach who should only see some clients.

    [Add and manage staff](/people/managing-staff) · [Staff access](/people/staff-access)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. What you sell

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set your hours">
    Go to **Scheduling → Setup → Availability** and click **New availability**. Make one template for each set of hours, such as "Weeknight lessons" or "Saturday rentals".

    [Create availability](/scheduling/create-your-availability)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your products">
    Go to **Payments → Products** and click **+ Create**. Most facilities start with a trial session, one or two memberships that grant credits each month, and a drop-in or package. Attach your waiver to each product so nobody can buy without agreeing.

    [Create a product](/payments/product-overview) · [Membership credits](/payments/credits/recurring-credit-memberships) · [Waivers](/tools/waivers/waivers)

    Not sure what to charge? See the [Pricing Your Services guide](/guides/pricing-your-services).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your schedulers">
    Go to **Scheduling → Schedulers** and click **Create scheduler**. A scheduler is one bookable service: its availability, how families pay (a price, credits or either), its location, coaches and spaces, and its booking rules. Preview it, then turn **Active** on.

    [Create and publish a scheduler](/scheduling/creating-your-first-scheduler)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Where families book

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what families fill in at sign-up">
    Go to **People → Sign-up & Profile** and pick the fields new families fill in, such as the athlete's birthday or school.

    [Sign-up & Profile](/people/sign-up-and-profile)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put your schedulers in your app">
    Go to **Website & App → App**, click **Edit app**, then **Schedule**. Add your schedulers, group them into sections, and click **Update**.

    [Schedule page in your app](/athlete-portal/schedule) · [Your app](/athlete-portal/understanding-your-athlete-portal)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build and publish your website">
    Go to **Website & App → Website** and click **Edit website**. Add a scheduling section so families can book from your site, then publish.

    [Your website](/website/overview) · [Scheduling sections](/website/section-types/scheduling)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 4. Bring your families in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Import your client list">
    Go to **Settings → Data → Import people**, download the template, and upload one row per person. Try five people first.

    [Import people](/settings/data#import-people)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move members onto their memberships">
    For families who already pay you, give them their membership or credits so they can book. Follow [Bring new families into CoachIQ](/flows/bring-in-new-families) for the rest of this step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 5. Check it end to end

Before you send your app link to everyone, book one session as a family would:

1. Open your app from **Website & App → App** (**Preview**) and sign in as a test client.
2. Book a free or credit session on each scheduler. Check the times, the price or credit cost, and the location.
3. Find the booking in **Scheduling → Calendar** under the right athlete.
4. Open the test client in **People → Clients** and check their credits and bookings.

A paid checkout is a real payment. Only use test cards if your Stripe account is in test mode.

If times don't show up, see [Scheduling troubleshooting](/scheduling/troubleshooting).
