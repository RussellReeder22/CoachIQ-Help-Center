A family can come in two ways: you add them, or they sign up themselves from your app or website. Either way, the steps after that are the same. Set up your [products](/payments/product-overview) and [schedulers](/scheduling/creating-your-first-scheduler) first; if you haven't, start with [Set up your business](/flows/set-up-your-business).

## 1. Get them into CoachIQ

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="They sign up">
    Send families your app's address. Copy it from **Website & App → App** (the copy button next to **Live**), or put a booking button on your website.

    New families create an account with the fields you chose in **People → Sign-up & Profile**. If you turn on **Ask for account type at sign-up**, a parent signs up as **Parent / Guardian** and adds their children. Most youth programs turn it on.

    [Your app](/app/overview) · [Sign-up & Profile](/people/sign-up-and-profile)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="You add them">
    Go to **People → Clients** and click **New client**. Pick **Athlete** for an adult or an athlete with their own login, or **Parent / Guardian** for a parent. Add children from the parent's profile: **Family → Add child**.

    Moving a whole list over? Use **Settings → Data → Import people** instead. Children without an email are added from the parent's **Family** section afterwards.

    [Add and edit clients](/people/adding-editing-clients) · [Import people](/settings/data#import-people) · [Family](/people/client-profiles/client-profiles#family)
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Tag new clients as you add them, for example `Fall 2026` or `Tuesday group`. Tags let you message, book and limit coaches to a group of clients later. See [Tags and filters](/people/tags-filters).
</Tip>

## 2. Get them signed in

People you added have no password yet. On their profile, click **Send set-password email** under **Sign-in**, or let them choose **Text me a code instead** on your website's sign-in. A child added from **Family** is **Managed · no login** until you click **Enable login**.

If someone gets stuck, [How clients sign in](/people/how-clients-sign-in) lists every way in and what to check.

## 3. Give them what they paid for

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="They buy it">
    Send them the product's link, or let them buy from your app or website. The membership, its credits and any waiver are handled at checkout.

    [Product links](/payments/product-builder#product-link) · [Membership credits](/payments/credits/recurring-credit-memberships)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="They already pay you">
    For families moving over with an existing membership, open their profile, go to **Products** and click **+ Add Product**. Paste their existing **Stripe Subscription ID** to link the real billing, or record a cash or check payment. No card is charged from this screen.

    [Manually adding products](/people/client-profiles/client-profiles-products#manually-adding-products)
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Check their balance on the **Credits** tab of their profile. See [Credits on a client profile](/people/client-profiles/client-profiles-credits).

## 4. Book their first session

- **They book it:** they open your app, go to **Sessions** (or whatever you've named your Schedule page) and pick a time. The scheduler takes payment or credits the way you set it up.
- **You book it:** in **Scheduling → Calendar**, click **Book session**, pick the athlete (the child, not the parent) and the scheduler, and turn on **Redeem credits** if the session should use their credits.

[Book a client yourself](/scheduling/manually-booking-athletes) · [Credits and schedulers](/scheduling/credits-and-schedulers)

## 5. Welcome them

- **Say hello.** Send a direct message from **Inbox → New message**. They see it in your app. See [Direct messages](/inbox/in-app-messages).
- **Automate it.** Build a welcome automation on the **Purchase Made** or **User Tag Added** trigger so every new family gets the same message without you remembering. Purchase automations don't run for products you add by hand, so use a tag for families you add yourself. See [Build your first automation](/tools/automations/build-your-first-automation).
- **Collect the rest.** If you need medical info or an intake questionnaire, attach a form to the product or send a form link. See [Forms](/tools/forms/overview).

## Check their first week

Open the client in **People → Clients**. Their profile shows the bookings, products, credits, waivers and messages in one place. **Activity** shows what they've done in your app. See [Client profiles](/people/client-profiles/client-profiles).
