Camps are open to anyone, not just your members, so your website is the storefront. Most camps need four things: a product for the fee, a form for registration, a page to send people to, and an automation for the messages. You don't need a scheduler if everyone shows up at the same time.

<Note>
  **Families pick days or time slots?** Then set the camp up as a scheduler instead. Choose **Camps & events** as its catalog type, charge per booking, and set **Max athletes per time slot** to your capacity. See [Create and publish a scheduler](/scheduling/creating-your-first-scheduler) and [Organize schedulers](/scheduling/organizing-schedulers).
</Note>

## 1. Create the camp product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a One-Time product">
    Go to **Payments → Products**, click **+ Create** and choose **One-Time**. Name it with the dates, for example "Summer Skills Camp, June 14–16", and put the times and what to bring in the description.

    [Create a product](/payments/product-overview) · [Product types](/payments/product-types)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cap the spots and add your waiver">
    In **Advanced**, turn on **Add a purchase limit for this product** and enter your number of spots. The camp shows **Sold out** when it's full. A per-user limit of 1 stops a family registering the same child twice. Turn on **Require Agreement to Terms** and pick your waiver so every parent agrees at checkout.

    [Advanced settings](/payments/product-builder#advanced) · [Require a waiver at checkout](/tools/waivers/waivers#require-a-waiver-at-checkout)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create">
    Save the product. Offering an early-bird price? Make a [coupon](/payments/coupon-overview) instead of a second product.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Build the registration form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the form">
    Go to **Tools → Forms** and click **Create form**. Ask only what you need on camp day: age or grade, emergency contact, shirt size, medical or allergy notes.

    [Create a form](/tools/forms/creating-forms)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the payment on the form">
    Open **Form settings → Connections** and switch on **Take a payment**. Click **2 · Products**, then **Add products**, and pick your camp product. Click **Save**.

    Families answer your questions, then pay. The payment adds them to your clients.

    [Take a payment on a form](/tools/forms/payments-and-bookings#take-a-payment)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Put it on your website

Go to **Website & App → Website**, click **Edit website** and add a page for the camp: dates, what athletes will work on, the price, and one **Register** call to action. Add a **Form** section and pick your registration form, then publish.

You can also share the form's own link from **Share** in the form's top bar, in texts, emails and social posts.

[Add a page](/website/pages-navigation/pages-navigation) · [Form section](/website/section-types/forms) · [Share a form](/tools/forms/sharing-forms)

## 4. Send the messages automatically

Go to **Tools → Automations** and click **Create**. A typical camp automation:

1. **When:** **Purchase Made**, with **Only if** set to **Is a specific product** and your camp product.
2. **Add a Tag to a User**, for example `Summer Camp 2026`. You'll use the tag to message everyone registered.
3. **Send an Email** with the confirmation: dates, times, address, what to bring.
4. **Wait/Delay** until the day before camp, then **Send an SMS Message** or **Send an Email** with the reminder.

[Build your first automation](/tools/automations/build-your-first-automation) · [Triggers and actions](/tools/automations/triggers-actions-reference)

## 5. Camp day

- **Your roster:** open the form and click **Responses** for every registration and its answers, or **Export CSV** for a printable list. Search **People → Clients** by the camp tag for who's registered.
- **Last-minute updates:** go to **Inbox → New message → Announcement** and send it to your camp tag. Parents of tagged athletes get it too.
- **Payments:** every camp payment is in **Payments → Transactions**.

[Form responses](/tools/forms/responses-and-automations) · [Announcements](/inbox/announcements) · [Transactions](/payments/transactions)

## After camp

Camp families are your best leads for memberships. Message the camp tag with your trial offer, or add them to your [Pipeline](/sales/pipeline) to follow up one by one. For what to charge for camps and clinics, see [Pricing Your Services](/guides/pricing-your-services#camps-clinics-and-team-training).
