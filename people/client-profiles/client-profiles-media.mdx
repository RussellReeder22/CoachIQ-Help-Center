<Info>
  **Where to find this**: open a client › **Profile** › **Media**
</Info>

**Media** shows the "videos and photos shared with" this client, and only theirs. Use it to check what a client has, or to add a clip from today's session straight to their profile.

- **Search** finds a video or photo by name.
- **Upload** adds a video or photo from your computer. It's shared with this client.
- To share something you already have, open it in [Media Hub](/content/media-hub) and share it from there.

A client with nothing yet shows **No media yet**: "Upload a video or photo, or share one from Media Hub."

Programs and lessons a client has access to aren't listed here; see their **Programs** activity in [All activity](/people/client-profiles/client-profiles-activity) or the program's own page.

<Note>
  Coaches whose staff access blocks **Media Hub** don't see the Media section on client profiles. See [Staff access](/people/staff-access#what-a-blocked-page-also-hides).
</Note>
