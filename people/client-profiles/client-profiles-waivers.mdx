<Info>
  **Where to find this**: Client Profile → **Waivers** (in the PROFILE group of tabs)
</Info>

## What it shows

The tab opens with a count such as **0/2 accepted**, then lists every active waiver in your account. A waiver the client has already agreed to shows its acceptance; one they have not shows an **Accept** button.

</Frame>

<Note>
  Labels were checked against the live app on 2026-09-02. The compliance report across all clients lives in **Tools → Waivers**; see [Waivers](/tools/waivers/waivers#track-waiver-compliance).
</Note>

## What to do here

- **Before a first session**, open the athlete's Waivers tab and confirm the count reads all accepted.
- **If a waiver is outstanding**, click **Message** in the profile header and send the link to a product or form that carries the waiver. They accept it as part of checkout or submission, and the tab updates.
- **If they signed in person**, on paper or verbally at the front desk, click **Accept** next to the waiver to record it on their profile. Keep the paper copy; the digital record shows the date you recorded it.
- **For a family**, check each connected child. Waivers are accepted per profile, so a parent's acceptance does not cover a child unless the product or form was completed for that child.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I mark a waiver accepted manually?">
    Yes. Click **Accept** next to the waiver on this tab. Use it when you collected the signature outside CoachIQ and want the profile to reflect it. Acceptances recorded at checkout or on a form carry the client's own action, which is stronger if a dispute ever arises.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A client bought a product but the waiver still shows as not accepted">
    The product does not have that waiver attached. Open the product's **Advanced** settings and check **Require Agreement to Terms**. See [Require a waiver at checkout](/tools/waivers/waivers#require-a-waiver-at-checkout).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I see everyone who is missing a waiver?">
    **Tools → Waivers**, click the acceptance count on the waiver, then filter by **Missing**. Export CSV from there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
