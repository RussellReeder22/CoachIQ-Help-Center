  **Where to find this**: Client Profile → **PROFILE** → **Waivers**
</Info>

## What it shows

Every waiver in your account and this client's status against it:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Waiver** | The waiver's label, as set in **Tools → Waivers** |
| **Status** | **Accepted** or **Missing** |
| **Accepted on** | The date and time they agreed |
| **Via** | Where they accepted: the product they bought or the form they submitted |

<Note>
  A screenshot of this tab is being captured. The compliance report across all clients lives in **Tools → Waivers** and is described in [Waivers](/tools/waivers/waivers#track-waiver-compliance).
</Note>

## What to do here

- **Before a first session**, open the athlete's Waivers tab and confirm the liability waiver shows **Accepted**.
- **If it shows Missing**, click **Message** in the profile header and send the link to a product or form that carries the waiver. They accept it as part of checkout or submission; you do not need to upload a signed PDF.
- **For a family**, check each connected child. Waivers are accepted per profile, so a parent's acceptance does not cover a child unless the product or form was completed for that child.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I mark a waiver accepted manually?">
    No. Acceptance is recorded when the client checks the box at checkout or on a form, so the record is defensible. If they signed on paper, keep the paper and have them accept the digital version at their next purchase.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A client bought a product but the waiver still shows Missing">
    The product does not have that waiver attached. Open the product's **Advanced** settings and check **Require Agreement to Terms**. See [Require a waiver at checkout](/tools/waivers/waivers#require-a-waiver-at-checkout).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I see everyone who is missing a waiver?">
    **Tools → Waivers**, click the acceptance count on the waiver, then filter by **Missing**. Export CSV from there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
