## Scheduling

**Move an entire session**

On a session click view on **Schedule** → **Calendar**, the header has **Move Session**. It moves the occurrence — every participant booking, coach, and resource together — to a new date and time in one action instead of rebooking each athlete. The move announcement names the original date and time and the new one, and you can edit it to add a personal note. [Learn more →](/scheduling/editing-rescheduling-bookings)

**Cancel entire session from the calendar**

On a session click view, the red trash next to **Move Session** opens **Cancel Session**. That closes the occurrence, cancels every current participant booking, and lets you notify with Announcement, SMS, or In-app message. [Learn more →](/scheduling/canceling-bookings)

**Edit duration from the calendar**

From a session on the calendar, **Duration** → **Edit** opens **Edit Duration**. Change **Duration in minutes** without changing the session date or start time. [Learn more →](/scheduling/editing-rescheduling-bookings)

**Per-date duration and resources on a session**

A single session can now carry a different length and different equipment on different dates — a 90-minute indoor practice on Tuesday and a 150-minute field practice on Saturday under one session, each with its own cage, mound, or turf. A date window shorter than the duration is now rejected on save instead of silently producing no bookable slots.

**Book by tag in New Booking**

On **Schedule** → **Calendar** → **+ Book Session**, the **Client** field lists **Tags** and **People**. Picking a tag adds a chip and checks people in your group who have that tag. [Learn more →](/scheduling/manually-booking-athletes)

**Allow resources to be double-booked on a scheduler**

In the scheduler builder, resources now have the same checkbox that connected coaches have: allow the attached resources to be booked at the same time by more than one session. Leave it off to keep double-booking prevention. [Learn more →](/scheduling/resources)

**Shared Calendar share modes**

When linking two groups under **Settings → Shared Calendar**, choose **All busy time** or **Resources only**. Resources only gives both groups one shared resource list, so a booking on a court or cage in either group blocks it for the other; busy time without a resource stays private. [Learn more →](/scheduling/shared-calendar-across-groups)

**Coach and resources in Day and Week view**

The calendar's **Day** and **Week** views now show the same session details as Month view — coach and attached resources — so you can see who is running a session and where without clicking into it. [Learn more →](/scheduling/calendar-overview-and-navigation)

**Copy scheduler link**

On **Schedule → Schedulers**, each card has a link icon with tooltip **Copy scheduler link**. [Learn more →](/scheduling/sharing-schedulers)

## Scheduling

**Schedulers catalog and Setup area**

**Schedule** is now **Calendar / Schedulers / Setup**. **Schedulers** is a searchable catalog: type chips (**Practices & games**, **Camps & events**, **Lessons**, **Rentals**, **Other**), a tag rail on the left, and cards showing type, duration, free/paid, and next availability. **Setup** holds the shared assets every scheduler attaches — **Availability**, **Locations**, **Resources**, **Coaches**, **Defaults** — each as a card grid with an **Add** control and **Edit / Duplicate**. The scheduler builder gains an **Organization** block (type plus tags) under **Details**. [Learn more →](/scheduling/organizing-schedulers)

**Scheduler Defaults**

**Setup → Defaults** pre-loads duration, buffers, scheduling window, booking rules, limits, reminders, and confirmations onto every new scheduler. Editing defaults does not rewrite schedulers you already created. [Learn more →](/scheduling/setup-defaults)

**Start cadence is separate from duration**

In a scheduler's **Advanced settings**, **Duration** and **New sessions can start** are independent, so a 60-minute lesson can offer a start every 30 minutes (9:00–10:00 or 9:30–10:30). **Allow overlapping sessions** is its own checkbox: leave it off and once 9:00 is booked, 9:30 drops out as a collision instead of putting two athletes in the same hour. [Learn more →](/scheduling/overlapping-slots)

**Free or Paywalled payment block**

The scheduler builder's **Payment** block starts with **Free** or **Paywalled**. Paywalled then lets families pay with a **price**, **credits**, or **either**. No new payment types — the same options, laid out so the choice is obvious. [Learn more →](/scheduling/setting-up-payment-options)

**A different venue for each session date**

Each date in a scheduler's availability can carry its own location, defaulting to the scheduler's location until you change it, so a team that practices Tuesdays at one field and Thursdays at another no longer needs a duplicate scheduler per venue. Athletes and parents see the per-date venue on their schedule, in the athlete portal, and in reminders. [Learn more →](/scheduling/setup-locations)

**Resources: Fixed or Any open**

In a scheduler's **Resources** block, choose how a space is picked: **Fixed** (every attached resource is required, the previous behavior) or **Any open** (the booking takes one free resource from the pool). Two hitting lessons at 4:00 can now each take a free cage without you building a scheduler per cage; staff see which resource was reserved. [Learn more →](/scheduling/resources)

**Connect coaches to individual sessions**

**Setup → Coaches → Connect coaches** lets you pick one or more coaches and one or more sessions in one step, so the coach on a practice or lesson can differ session to session instead of being fixed for the whole scheduler. [Learn more →](/scheduling/setup-coaches)

**Share a calendar across two groups**

Organization owners can link two groups under the same organization from **Settings → Shared Calendar** so a booking in one group blocks the same time in the other, in both directions. Built for facilities that run, say, a baseball group and a softball group out of one building. Unlink at any time. [Learn more →](/scheduling/shared-calendar-across-groups)

**Conflict warning on manual recurring bookings**

When you add a recurring booking from the admin calendar and it overlaps another booking on the same session, CoachIQ now tells you which booking it collides with and lets you confirm or back out, instead of silently double-booking a capacity-1 session. [Learn more →](/scheduling/manually-booking-athletes)

Track attendance by [checking in athletes](/scheduling/checking-in-athletes) when they arrive for sessions. Click any booking on your calendar to see the participant list, then click the checkmark next to each athlete to mark them as checked in.

- Check-in timestamps are saved to each booking
- View check-in data in calendar list view or on client profiles
- Check-in data exports with your scheduling data
- Easily undo accidental check-ins with one click

## Automations: Major Upgrade

We shipped a significant update to [Automations](/tools/automations/what-are-automations) this month, giving you more power to automate your business workflows.

### New Features

**Session Notes & Custom Fields in Automations**

You can now include session notes and athlete [custom fields](/people/adding-editing-clients#adding-custom-fields) (like skill level, position, or training focus) in your automated messages. Perfect for sending personalized session reminders with context about each athlete.

**Booking Notes**

Add notes to individual sessions or entire recurring booking series. Great for tracking athlete-specific information, session goals, or occurrence-specific details. Notes are visible from your [calendar](/scheduling/calendar-overview-and-navigation) and can be used in automations.

**Wait/Delay Actions**

Create timed sequences in your automations. For example: send a welcome email immediately, then follow up with a training tips message 3 days later. [See all available actions →](/tools/automations/triggers-actions-reference#all-actions)

**Scheduled Check Trigger**

Run automations on a recurring schedule (daily, weekly, etc.) to check conditions across your athlete base. Use this to automatically tag athletes based on subscription status, send weekly check-ins, or manage credits. [Learn more about scheduled automations →](/tools/automations/scheduled-automations)

- Fixed announcement sending errors
- Wait/delay duration can now be adjusted properly
- Trash icon now works correctly under wait/delay conditions
- Product list UI no longer overlaps in automation builder
- Scheduled check trigger now saves and fires correctly
